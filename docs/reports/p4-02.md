**Inputs:** the primary family's rows at t and t−7 (`v7`, `v7_prev`, `g7`, `g7_prev`), plus `days_observed`, `has_gap`, `age_days` and `delisted_all`.

**No stage (None + reason), checked in this order:**
1. `no source`: `days_observed` is 0.
2. `dead · delisted`: every listing is delisted. This is a fact, so it's checked before history.
3. `insufficient history`: fewer than 15 days.
4. `gap`: either row is flagged.
5. `insufficient history`: a NULL V7(t), V7(t−7) or G7(t) without a gap.

**Rules, first match wins:**
1. dead · no sales: at most 1 unit in each of two weeks.
2. declining · two weeks down: −20% at t and t−7.
3. declining · sharp drop: −36% at t.
4. peaking · braking: +20% or more, with acceleration ≤ −0.25.
5. peaking · plateau: under +20%.
6. emerging · new and growing: +20% or more, age ≤ 28 days and at least 3 units.
7. rising · growing.

**Departures from the planning note (all in DEC-307):**
- Rule 3b ("stalled after a run") is folded into the plateau. It gives the same stage and needs the stored stage history.
- A gap flag gives no stage, even when DEC-275 tolerates the gap for V7.
- Hysteresis is left to the scores writer.
