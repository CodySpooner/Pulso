## What changed

- **API (`routers/boards.py`, +65 lines):**
  - `BoardItem` gains `merged_into_id`, `currency`, `price_min_minor`, `price_max_minor`, `best_margin_minor`, `trend`, `saturation` and `lifecycle`.
  - `with_stats()` builds them for a page, and for the item PATCH answer, from the reads that already exist:
    - Discover's `latest_snapshots` + `listing_stats` for the price range;
    - Discover's `best_margins` + `margin_stat` (the `workspace_product_economics` view) for the best landed margin. Nothing re-derives it, so imp-520's listing choice flows through;
    - `pulso_ml.scores.opportunity.latest_rows` (imp-488) + the Screener's `score_value` for the three scores.
  - A merged product resolves through the watchlists' `resolve()` (any chain). The card keeps its own product and shows the survivor's stats (DEC-344 b).
  - **9 queries per page, for 1 card or 50.**
- **Generated:** `apps/api/openapi.json` and `apps/web/src/lib/api/schema.d.ts` (`pnpm gen:api`).
- **Web (`boards/[id]/stats.tsx` new; `cards.tsx`, `board.tsx`, `model.ts`):**
  - **Grid card:** a stat list under the title: Price, Best margin, Trend, Lifecycle chip, Saturation, Data as of. Labels left, tabular numbers right.
  - **Table:** columns Price, Best margin, Trend (right-aligned), Lifecycle, Saturation (right-aligned) and Data as of. It fits at 1440px with the row menu.
  - **Provenance:** every stat opens where it came from on hover, keyboard focus and tap. Prices and margin use the shared `Metric` card. Scores get a card with "Pulso score · {model}", the UTC day scored and the as-of.
  - **Missing values:** "No source yet", "Insufficient signal" and "Duty not set" per stat, never 0.
  - **Stale:** "Data as of" is the oldest stat's age, with an amber dot and "· stale" when a traced stat is stale.
  - **Merged note:** "Merged into another product; its stats show here. Open it" links to the survivor. It sits under the title in the grid and in the Note cell in the table.
  - **Loading:** the card skeleton gains the stat rows.
- **Fixtures:** `e2e/boards/fixtures.ts` gives every fixture product stats covering each state (values, a loss, Duty not set, No source yet, Insufficient signal, stale, merged), plus a `holdItems` option for the loading screenshot.
- **Size:** 313 added production lines excluding generated files (API 65, web 248), under the 400 target.
