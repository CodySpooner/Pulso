### Outside the listed ownership: `pulso_core.scores`
The brief asks for `explanation.panel_size`, but `Explanation` refuses unknown keys. Score Definitions §3 says a score that needs more fields "adds them as optional fields in its own PR", and it names Saturation's `panel_size` as an example. So I added it there, together with `Component.normalization`: Score Definitions §4 asks for the fallback level in the explanation. Both default to None:
- earlier explanations still validate;
- `write()` stores them in the existing jsonb with no migration;
- `packages/core/tests/test_scores.py` passes against Postgres (16 passed, none skipped).

`/methodology`'s contract tables pick up the three new fields from the export, so no web change was needed.

## Behaviour

`saturation_scores(rows, categories)`:
- `rows` are day t's `shp_sellers` rows. Both `Feature` and the ORM `ProductDailyFeature` satisfy `Row`; I checked the ORM one under mypy with a temporary file inside the package and then deleted it.
- `categories` maps every product to score to its lineage: its category, then each ancestor, nearest first. An empty lineage means the product reads global.

| Component | Raw | Weight | Source | When missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `shp_sellers` | ln(1 + s), s over every tracked store | 0.40 | `shopify_stores` | Required: no value, with a reason |
| `shp_seller_g28` | as stored: growth over the stable panel | 0.25 | `shopify_stores` | Renormalised; reads "insufficient signal" |
| `ads_active` | — | 0.25 | none | Always; reads "no source yet" |
| CJ listings | — | not configured | — | Left out of v0 (DEC-318 c) |

- **Value:** T = Σ w_eff × z from `pulso_core.scores.explain()`. Saturation = clamp(50 + 50 × T / 3, 0, 100).
- **Bands:** low below 35, high above 65, medium between, with both cut-offs medium.
- **Robust z:** taken within the category, falling back up the lineage when n < 30 or MAD = 0, then to global, which is used whatever its n. With no spread anywhere there is no z, never a guessed one. The cohort is recorded per component as `normalization: {category_id, n}`.
- **No value, with its reason, never 0:** `no seller count` (no row, or a version-001 row) and `seller counts don't vary`. `value`, `band` and `explanation` are then None.
- **Explanation:** `panel_size` comes from the row. The growth component's `gap` is set when the row lists missed runs in t−28..t.

**Coverage, and the vault's numbers.** The lamp scores 73.72, the vault's "without CJ" 73.7. Its coverage is 0.65 / 0.90 = 0.72, not the vault's 0.65, because v0 doesn't configure CJ (DEC-318 c). The lead may want the worked example's coverage line to say so (see the vault lines below).

**Coverage-drift ceiling (DEC-318 h).** The ten-new-stores test holds exactly: every untouched product's score and explanation is identical. That's because growth reads the stable panel, and the median and MAD are rank statistics that a few touched products can't move. A batch that re-ranks a whole category, with many products crossing its median or MAD, would still move untouched products' z. That's what normalising against the category means, so I recorded it rather than built around it. There's a backlog row below.

**Acceptance, case by case:**
- **The vault worked example without CJ gives 73.7:** 41 Lamps are placed on the vault's medians and MADs. The result is 73.72 (high), z 1.24 and 1.72, `missing: ["ads_active"]`, `panel_size` 12, cohort `{Lamps, 41}`. The points sum back to the value, and the explanation round-trips through JSON.
- **Ten newly tracked stores leave untouched products unchanged:** 31 lamps, 8 panel stores, and 10 stores first seen yesterday listing the most crowded lamp, all run through the real `seller_inputs()`. The 30 untouched products' `Saturation` tuples are equal. The listed lamp's level goes from ln 9 to ln 19, while its growth component and `panel_size` (8) don't change.
- **A missing ads input renormalises and is listed in `missing`:** ads has `weight_eff` 0, `contribution` None and `source` None. Missing growth renormalises onto the level (`weight_eff` 1.0, coverage 0.40 / 0.90).
- **No seller row gives insufficient signal, never 0:** no row, a version-001 row and flat seller counts each give `(None, None, None, reason)`.
- **A one-product category falls back to the parent, then global:** six fallback-chain cases. n = 30 keeps its category; n = 29 and a one-product category use the parent; a small parent uses the next ancestor; every level small uses global.
- **MAD = 0 falls back:** with n = 30 the parent is used, and a flat global gives no z.
- **A weights change changes the model_version:** changing `shp_sellers`, `ads_active`, `min_n`, `high_above` or `revision` each gives `version(CONFIG)`, which differs from `VERSION`.
- **Also covered:** winsorization at ±3 and at ±4 with `cap` 4; the band edges 34.99, 35, 65 and 65.01; the clamp at 0 and 100; a row for a product not asked for staying out of both the category and the global cohort.
- **The methodology drift test passes:** after regenerating, `test_the_committed_export_is_the_code` is green.
