**Overview tab.** Five cards, each number a `<Metric>` with its provenance card:

- **Price range:** min–max of current USD retail across live store listings, with the median below.
- **Best supplier cost:** the lowest in-stock unit cost, with its platform.
- **Landed margin:** a designed "No source yet" until UX-8.
- **First seen:** the UTC date, with its age in days.
- **Velocity 7d:** the best-graded family, with a grade chip, a "lower bound" chip for B, a gap chip, and G7 momentum shown with a sign and ▲/▼. With no qualifying family it says "Insufficient signal".

Trend Score, Saturation and Lifecycle are not rendered (`ff_scores`, Phase 4), and the grid reflows: 2-up on phones, 3 + 2 from 768 px, 5 in a row from 1920 px.

"Where it appears" lists counts per source: CJ suppliers and Shopify stores, each with a button that opens the Suppliers or Sellers tab, and ads as "No source yet". Numbers are right-aligned in tabular numerals.

**Context rail.** Beside every tab from 1280 px, and stacked under the tab panel below that.

- **Tags:** chips with keyboard remove buttons, and Enter in the tag box adds a tag. A case-only repeat is refused inline.
- **Notes:** a list, newest first, with UTC timestamps. N focuses the box and Ctrl/⌘ + Enter adds the note. Older notes load in pages of 20.
- **Saves:** both are optimistic and roll back on error with the reason and the error ID. A failed note's text goes back into the box.

**States, every new surface:**

- loading skeleton (the exact grid);
- empty (no tags, no notes);
- error with Retry and the error ID;
- stale (a stale line when a refresh fails, and per-card "· stale" with a ⚠ glyph);
- no source yet / insufficient signal, never 0.

## Acceptance checks

### (a) Five products' Overview stats equal the SQL spot check

`test_overview_stats_match_sql_spot_checks` builds five fixture products, then compares every overview stat with an independent raw-SQL query (latest snapshot per listing with `DISTINCT ON`, `percentile_disc(0.5)` for the median, and the family ranking with `array_position`). The five products are:

- **full:** USD and CAD stores and a delisted store; CJ offers that are cheapest-but-unavailable, at stock 0, and in stock; `shp_inv` and `shp_bump` features on the same day, plus an older day;
- **unmatched:** no listings;
- **even:** two prices, so the median is the lower middle;
- **out of stock:** offers only;
- **single:** one USD store, a CAD offer, a delisted cheap offer, and only `cj_stock`.

`test_overview_traces_each_stat` checks the payload ids, sources, `stale`, grade and growth, and the appearance order.

### (b) A note added by workspace A is invisible to B

`test_workspace_b_never_sees_or_edits_a` (`apps/api/tests/test_product_notes.py`): B sees no notes and no tags, and B's PUT doesn't touch A's tags; the database holds only A's tag. A mutation check confirmed that the test fails when the workspace filter is removed.

### (c) Designed states

- the loading skeleton;

- per-card stale;
- no source yet: 4 cards and 3 appearance rows, with no `$0` or `0` anywhere in the panel;
- the rail: empty, load errors, and the rollback after a failed save.
