# imp-154 + imp-186 + imp-187 — Offline reasons name their action; an open reason covers nothing

**Agent:** Claude (Orca worker, `task_82065f35fc3f`, dispatch `ctx_d35bc1093e05`) · **Branch:** `CodySpooner/imp-154-tooltip-reasons` (rebased on `4d7a9ee`, which has imp-171) · **Decision:** DEC-235 (a, b, c), proposed (imp-154) · **Vault:** Agents/Improvement Backlog rows imp-154, imp-186, imp-187; Features/Data Health · **Sources:** `docs/reports/imp-134.md` leftover 2, `docs/reports/imp-173.md` leftovers 1 and 2

## Summary

1. **imp-154: every offline reason names its action.** One helper, `offlineHint(action)` in `components/shell/offline-banner.tsx`, gives "You're offline. Reconnect to <action>." Each of the 7 call sites passes its visible verb, plus the source when the control acts on one source (DEC-235 a). `OFFLINE_HINT` had no users left, so it is deleted.
2. **imp-186: a stale line's Retry reason opens where the line is empty.** `StaleLine` reads its own layout with a `ResizeObserver` (DEC-235 b):
   - **above** while Retry starts the line's first row;
   - **on Retry's left** once the actions wrap onto a row of their own;
   - **below** otherwise.

   A reason beside its trigger wraps at 128 px, so it fits in the empty space left of Retry at 375. This is one rule for every stale line (Coverage, Sources, the host and backup cards, the page line and the run drawer's), and the drawer needs no special case.
3. **imp-187: a phone card's reasons open below the controls row.** That covers the switch and Run now. The phone cards and their skeleton go from `py-3` to `py-4`, because 25 px between cards can't hold a 26 px reason plus its 6 px offset (DEC-235 c). The table keeps top.

What is locked, when, and the DEC-232 press and one-at-a-time behaviour are unchanged.

### Why imp-186 isn't the brief's two-way rule

The brief's rule (above when nothing wrapped above Retry, else below) fixes 1440. At 375, though, the reason below Retry still covered the top 5 px of the coverage chart's first row: the "Shopify stores" label and 24 cells. The room under a wrapped line is 27 px, and the reason needs 32. Above is no better, because the Error ID line sits there; with a real uuid4 request ID it fills the row. The only empty spot next to Retry at 375 is the left part of its own row, 130 px wide. So the third side is "left", with the reason wrapped onto two lines ("You're offline." / "Reconnect to retry.", 104 px of text, a 128 px box).

### Why imp-187 changes the card padding

Below Run now there are 25 px before the next card's name: 12 px of padding, the divider, then 12 px. The reason needs 32 px: a 6 px offset, which clears the 4 px focus ring (2 px outline, 2 px offset), plus the 26 px reason. With `side="bottom"` alone, the reason's last 2 px covered the next card's name glyphs (6 px into its line box). Left of Run now is Check CSV, and a smaller offset would sit on the focus ring. `py-4` gives 33 px. The skeleton cards get the same padding, so nothing moves when the data lands (imp-082).
