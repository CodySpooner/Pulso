### Root cause

**A product bug, the one behind backlog row imp-156, not an imp-145 change and not a wrong test.** Radix Tooltip closes whenever an ancestor of its trigger scrolls (a capture-phase `scroll` listener on `window`). At lg, `main` is the scroll container. Focusing a control that is partly or fully out of view scrolls `main`, and that scroll's event is dispatched on the next frame, after the focus has already opened the reason. So the reason opens and closes within one frame: the control keeps focus, but `aria-describedby` goes and `data-state` turns `closed`.

The spec scrolled each control into view before focusing it, to work around exactly this. That only narrowed the race. `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded` moved `main` from 95 to 76 px to reveal Refresh, and its scroll event landed about 7 ms after Playwright returned. Whenever that event, or a focus-driven scroll, landed after `focus()`, the test failed with the lead's signature.

Evidence, from an instrumented throwaway spec (capture-phase `scroll`, `focusin`/`focusout`, `tooltip.open` and `data-state` logging; deleted):
- **Same scroll offsets with or without imp-145.** `main` scrolls to 946 (file check), 95 (`cj.click`) and 76 (Refresh), both with imp-145's `coverage.tsx` and with `origin/main`'s, so imp-145 doesn't change the layout or timing here. The lead's 4/4 was this latent race. I could not reproduce that exact timing: the unchanged spec passed 4/4 plain, 3/3 at 6× CPU throttle and 3/3 with 16 busy cores.
- **Two deterministic triggers give the lead's exact error.** (A) scroll `main` and focus Refresh in one task; (B) focus Refresh without scrolling first, so `focus()` itself scrolls `main` 95 → 76. Both fail 2/2 with "3 × locator resolved to … `data-state="closed"` … `data-radix-popper-side="top"`", then `closed`. Refresh is still the active element.
- **Ruled out:**
  - the Disable dialog's late focus return (it lands about 170 ms after `toBeHidden`, before Refresh's focus);
  - another tooltip opening (no `tooltip.open` after Refresh's);
  - a remount (the same element throughout).

### Fix (product, general: also closes imp-156)

`apps/web/src/components/data-health/atoms.tsx`, `TapTooltip`:
- **Stays open through a scroll while focused:** `onOpenChange` ignores a close that Radix requests during a `scroll` event (`window.event?.type === "scroll"`) while the trigger is `document.activeElement`. Blur, Escape, a press, another tooltip opening, and hover reasons on scroll or pointer leave all close as before.
- **`hideWhenDetached` on its content:** while the trigger is scrolled out of its scroll container, the reason is hidden (it stays the trigger's description) instead of floating over the top bar, and it comes back with the trigger.

Every TapTooltip gets this: Refresh, the source switches, Run now, Choose CSV… and the status pills. The alternatives are in DEC-213.
