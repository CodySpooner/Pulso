### The tooltip carries no link

The DEC-196 hint lives in a Radix Tooltip (`role="tooltip"`). It is not a place for a control:
- The ARIA APG says a tooltip never takes focus.
- TapTooltip's content is click-through on purpose, so an open hint never covers a control.
- The tooltip closes on blur, and its content is portalled, so Tab from the pill never reaches it.

A link in the hint would therefore be unreachable by keyboard. Making it reachable would take an interactive tooltip or a toggletip: about 30 lines of focus handling in the TapTooltip that every reason uses. So the hint keeps its text ("…Retry on the Collector host card."), and the 1-click link sits in persistent text in two places:
- the "Gap check didn't load" note right under the pill;
- the Open gaps line.

The e2e checks that Tab moves on from the pill and closes the hint.
