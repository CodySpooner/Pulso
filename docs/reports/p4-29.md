## Behaviour (DEC-342)

- **The day:**
  - The URL holds it as `?date=YYYY-MM-DD`. With no date, the page shows the API's latest scored day.
  - Previous, Next and **Latest** push the URL, so Back steps back a day. The button is "Latest", not "Today", because the default day is usually yesterday (UTC).
  - The header says which UTC day is shown, and either "the latest scored day" or "as the data stood when the day closed".
  - While a new day loads, the old cards dim to 50% (opacity only).
- **Cards:**
  - Order: alerts first when any are unread, then the populated sections, then the no-source ones.
  - Each card shows its own "As of" (the UTC time on the day, otherwise the date). Hover, focus or tap shows the newest input and the sources.
  - States:
    - **ok:** up to 10 rows;
    - **empty:** the API's reason, and the empty watchlist offers Discover;
    - **no source:** a dashed card with "No source yet." plus the reason and Open Data Health. Never a number;
    - **stale:** the as of is more than 36 h before the cutoff, shown with an amber dot and the word for screen readers.
- **Rows:**
  - The whole row opens the product page, on the tab that fits the section.
  - The row's hover title gives the source and as of.
  - Deltas are DeltaBadges, with a sign and ▲/▼.
- **Movers:** an Up/Down segmented control in the header (one click). Values show old → new.
- **Watchlist:**
  - A list picker appears when there is more than one list.
  - Each row has a 14-day sparkline from `series`, which breaks at unscored days and shows a dashed baseline under 3 points.
  - Each row has the stage pill (a dot plus a title when the stage was entered on the day), the day's alert chips, and Trend since yesterday ("—" with its reason when there is no comparable score).
  - The footer opens the list on `/watchlists`.
- **Alerts (imp-510):**
  - The card lists the day's alerts, unread first, with the unread count.
  - Each alert opens its product's Suppliers tab, and the footer opens `/alerts`.
  - The card doesn't mark alerts read (DEC-330).
- **Page states:**
  - loading skeleton;
  - error with Retry;
  - a failed refresh keeps the day under a StaleLine;
  - a latest day before yesterday shows "Nothing scored since …";
  - nothing ok on the latest day shows the first-run card.

## Design review (styleseed-design-review)

- **Round 0: 85/100 (B).**
  - Top emerging had status colour on every row: every product there is emerging or rising, so the green pill said nothing (−4).
  - The stale dot was colour alone (−2).
  - No-source cards were tall centred slabs, six identical ones on an empty database (−3).
  - The empty watchlist had no action (−2).
  - "as of" in the card headers vs "As of" elsewhere (−1).
- **Round 1 (all applied): 96/100 (A).** The changes:
  - the stage pill is neutral in Top emerging;
  - the stale state is worded for screen readers;
  - no source is a one-line status in a dashed card;
  - the empty watchlist offers "Find products in Discover";
  - "As of" is capitalised.
- **Left (−4, below any fix threshold):**
  - card icons use stroke 1.5 while pill icons use 2, matching the system's `Pill`;
  - eight list cards share one anatomy, which is the note's layout.
