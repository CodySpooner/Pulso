## Behaviour

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **`GET /v1/watchlists/{id}/items?spark=velocity&days=30`** | Each item gets `spark`: 30 points `{day, value}` from yesterday (the newest UTC day whose cutoff has passed) back, oldest first. `value` is the stored V7 of the row's own velocity family; a day with no row or a gap V7 is `null`. `value`, `as_of` and `stale` are the newest valued point's, with the source, method and `raw_payload_id: null`. No value in any family in the window gives `{"value": null, "reason": "No velocity in the last 30 days."}`. |
| **Without `spark`** | The page is unchanged: no `spark` key, the same fields and the same cursor. |
| **Batch** | Two statements for the whole page, whatever its size. |
| **`/watchlists`, 1440px** | A "30 days" column after Velocity. Hover, focus or tap opens the card: "Velocity, last 30 days", Source, As of, Stale, Method, Raw payload, then "No data Oct 2 → Oct 4, Oct 15" when the line breaks. The accessible name reads, for example, "Velocity, last 30 days: 29/7d to 77/7d, no data Oct 2 → Oct 4, Oct 15". No series reads "No source yet" in muted text, and its card gives the reason. |
| **`/watchlists`, phone** | Each card shows the sparkline (or "No source yet") under its `…` menu, right-aligned. A tap opens the card, not the product. |
| **Loading** | Each skeleton row gains a flat grey line where the sparkline will be. Empty, error and stale-refetch states are the list's own; a stale row's line ends early, its card says Stale, and its Last seen dot is amber. |

**Clicks:** a watched product's 30-day velocity trend now takes **0** clicks: it is on the list when it loads. Its source, as of and gaps take **1** hover, focus or tap. Before, it took 2 clicks (the product, then its Trends tab) for each product.

**Acceptance, item by item:**
- **100 items, 30 points each, fixed statements:**
  - A 100-item page returns 30 dated points on every row with a series.
  - `statements(limit=100, spark) == statements(limit=1, spark) == statements(limit=100) + 2`.
- **A missing day is null, not 0, not interpolated:**
  - p1 has no rows 3 and 4 days back and a gap V7 5 days back. Exactly those three days are `null`.
  - The newest feature version wins (999).
  - CJ rows (7.0) never appear in p1's `shp_inv` line.
  - p2's two trailing gaps are `null`, and its `as_of` is 2 days earlier.
  - p3 has only gap rows and reads `NoSource`.
- **Another workspace's list:** 404.
- **Without spark:** the page equals the spark page minus `spark`, with the same `next_cursor`.
- **`days`:** 7 gives 7 points. 0, 91 and `spark=price` are 422s.
- **p95, 100 items:**
  - The data is synthetic and dev-sized, in a `perf` schema of the same throwaway container: 3,000 products, 2 families × 120 days (720,000 feature rows, about 5% gap V7s), and 100 random products on the list.
  - Over 100 requests through the in-process TestClient: **p95 214 ms** (p50 95, max 259) with `spark=velocity`, against the **300 ms** target. Without `spark`: p95 58 ms.
  - The script is a scratch file, not committed; the schema was dropped afterwards.
- **Vitest:** the shared Sparkline draws 2 polylines for `[1, 2, 3, null, null, 4, 5, 6]`, a dot for a lone value between gaps, and a dashed baseline under 3 values. The Daily Pulse watchlists card still renders `<polyline`.
- **Playwright on fixtures:**
  - At 1440px the "30 days" column is visible with no horizontal scroll in the table.
  - Product 3's line is 3 polylines. Hover and keyboard focus each open a card with Shopify stores, As of and "No data Oct 2 → Oct 4, Oct 15".
  - Product 5 shows "No source yet" and has no polyline.
  - At 375px the phone cards show the sparkline and "No source yet", with no horizontal page scroll, and a tap opens the card and stays on `/watchlists`.
