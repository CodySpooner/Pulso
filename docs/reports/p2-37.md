Task p2-37 (owner priority 2026-10-25 07:34Z, Lead State Queue 000b piece 1 rest, p2-33 backlog row b1). Decision: DEC-277. Branch `CodySpooner/p2-37-brand-hide-list`, rebased onto `origin/main` at `19dc46e` (p2-33, p2-35 part a, imp-278 and p2-36 merged; still no migration past 0010).

## What was built

- **Migration 0011, `popular_brand_rules`** (`PopularBrandRule` in `packages/core/src/pulso_core/models/core.py`, `WorkspaceOwned`):
  - columns: `workspace_id` (NOT NULL, indexed), `brand_norm`, `brand` (the spelling, for display), `action` (`hide` | `show`), `created_at` and `created_by`;
  - primary key `(workspace_id, brand_norm)`;
  - two CHECKs: `action IN ('hide','show')` and `brand_norm = lower(brand)`, the same key DEC-272 counts major brands by;
  - it holds current preference, not history: an upsert replaces a rule and a remove is a plain DELETE;
  - the downgrade refuses while any rule exists ("restore a backup"), like 0005 does for owner input.
- **API** (`apps/api/src/pulso_api/routers/popular_products.py`, workspace-scoped, stable operation ids):
  - `list_popular_brand_rules`: `GET /v1/popular-products/brands`, keyset-paged on `brand_norm`;
  - `put_popular_brand_rule`: `PUT /v1/popular-products/brands` with `{brand, action}`, an upsert;
  - `delete_popular_brand_rule`: `DELETE /v1/popular-products/brands?brand=`, which returns the rule removed or null.
  - `GET /v1/popular-products` now applies the caller's rules:
    - a hidden brand is left out and counted in `hidden` and in the new `hidden_by_you`;
    - a shown brand is never hidden by the 10-row rule;
    - with `include_major_brands=true` every row comes back with `hidden_by`: `major`, `you` or null.
- **Page** (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/google-popular/`):
  - **Row action.** Each brand cell has a one-click icon button:
    - **Hide brand** on a visible row;
    - **Unhide brand** on a row you hid, seen with hidden brands shown;
    - **Always show brand** on a major-brand row, also seen with hidden brands shown.
    - It appears on row hover or focus and always on touch. It's a real button, keyboard reachable, with the global focus ring.
  - **Chips:** Major, Hidden (yours) and Shown (a major brand you show).
  - **Toolbar and summary.** The toggle reads "Show hidden brands (N)", and the count line splits major brands from yours: "25 products, 15 hidden: 10 from major brands, 5 by you".
  - **Optimistic update with a clear undo:**
    - the brand's loaded rows leave at once, or are re-flagged when every brand is shown;
    - a polite toast says what changed and offers **Undo** for 8 s (held while hovered or focused);
    - the report then refetches;
    - if the change fails, the rows come back and the toast says why.
  - **Focus.** When a hidden row's button leaves with its row, focus moves to the next row's button.
- **Fewest clicks:** hiding a brand takes **1 click**. Unhiding takes **2** (Show hidden brands, then Unhide), and Undo in the toast is 1.

**Size.** 416 added lines of production code (router +133, migration 61, model and export 18, page +82 and description 1, brands.tsx 95, model.ts 26), excluding tests, fixtures, generated files, DECISIONS and screenshots. About 460 lines are tests and fixtures.

**Manage brands moved to the backlog (brief's fallback).** The Manage brands popover was built and tested in commit `eaecafe`, then removed in `af6f855`, because production code had reached about 535 lines:
- what it did: listed hidden and always-shown brands, unhid with one click, updated optimistically with focus kept in the list, and had loading, empty, error and stale states (3 Playwright state tests);
- to restore it: `git revert af6f855` brings back the popover, its fixtures and its tests;
- until then, a brand you hid comes back only from a report that lists it (backlog row b1 below). The list API is built and tested.
