## UI bar

- **styleseed-design-review** (board, drawer, kill dialog, Start a test): about 91 on the first pass, then **97 (A)** after one fix round.
  - First pass: −4 writing (the drawer's button said "Save"), −4 hierarchy (ROAS "×" at the number's size), −1 hierarchy (card stats all one weight).
  - Fixed: "Save metrics"; the drawer's "×" is a step smaller and muted.
  - Also fixed from the screenshots: the 2×2 stats ran together in the narrow five-column cards, so a container query now puts one stat per row under 15 rem; the stale pill overflowed its card, so it now wraps as one sentence; the drawer focused the Ad spend field on open, which would open a phone keypad, so the sheet itself takes focus; the menu items are 44 px under a coarse pointer.
  - Left at 97: card stats keep one weight. It's a dense data view (AGENTS §8), so no backlog row.
- **States:**
  - Loading: five column skeletons with card skeletons (`role=status`).
  - Empty: "No tests yet. Start one from a product page", with "Find a product in Discover".
  - Error: `PanelError` with the cause, error ID and Retry.
  - Stale: a failed refresh keeps the board, with `StaleLine` "Couldn't refresh your tests" and its as-of time.
  - No source yet: null CPA or ROAS shows `Missing`.
  - Missing economics: the drawer's "Set price and supplier…" line.
  - Stale metrics: the amber pill.
- **Keyboard and motion:**
  - Every card has a title button (Enter opens the drawer) and a Move button, both with the focus ring.
  - The menu and dialog are Radix, so arrow keys, Enter and Escape work; focus follows a moved card.
  - Motion: shared `layoutId` with `spring.smooth` for menu moves, an instant landing for drops, no layout animation in a column of more than 50 cards, and the drag overlay at scale 1.02 with `--shadow-popover`.
  - `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` (the shell); the reduced-motion e2e passes. Nothing is virtualized.
- **Contrast and tokens:** tokens only. Killed cards keep full opacity: the vault's 60% fade would put their text below AA (DEC-315 i). Status is never colour alone: the stale pill has a clock icon and words, and counts are numbers.
- **Clicks (main flow):**
  - Product Page → Start a test (1) → Open the board (1): you land on the board with the new test's drawer open.
  - Moving a card is 2 clicks (Move, then a stage); Killed adds 1 (the reason). Or one drag, plus the reason for Killed.
  - Logging metrics is 1 click to open the card, the edits, then Save metrics (1).
- **Screenshots** (`docs/screenshots/p4-08/`, fixture data under the E2E auth bypass, no database; each at `-1440-dark`, `-1440-light`, `-375-dark`, `-375-light`):
  - `board-*`: the board with all five columns.
  - `drawer-*`: Posture corrector brace, with the full start snapshot.
  - `drawer-no-source-*`: Galaxy star projector, with an incomplete snapshot and null CPA.
  - `kill-reason-*`: the kill-reason dialog.
  - `move-menu-*`: the Move to… menu.
  - `loading-*`, `empty-*`, `error-*`, `stale-*`: the states.
  - `start-test-*`: the Product Page after Start a test.
