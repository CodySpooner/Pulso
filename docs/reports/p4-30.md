## Behaviour

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **`GET /v1/tests?format=csv`** | Every test of the workspace matching `stage`, newest first, with no cursor or limit, as `pulso_testing_<date>.csv`. Columns: `id, product_id, product, stage, started_on, ended_on, currency, ad_spend, revenue, orders, cpa, roas, break_even_cpa, stop_loss, signal, kill_reason, notes, created_at, updated_at`. CPA, ROAS, break-even, stop-loss and the signal come from `view()`, the same function as the JSON. Money is in dollars. No spend or no orders gives empty cells. |
| **`GET /v1/screener?format=csv`** | The filtered set in the requested `sort` and `order`, with no cursor or limit, as `pulso_screener_<date>.csv`. Columns: `id, product, category, lifecycle, velocity_7d, velocity_grade, acceleration, trend, saturation, opportunity, margin, margin_pct, price, currency, supplier_rating, ship_days_us, as_of, stale`. Rows come from `items()`, the JSON page's builder. Risk filters are not applied; `X-Unapplied` names them as `phase_5`. |
| **Both** | The BOM, ISO 8601 UTC with `Z`, the count-first 50k cap (422 `export_too_large`, no file), streamed 500 rows a fetch, `X-Total-Count`, the formula guard on text |
| **Testing Board** | "9 tests" (or, past 100, "Your newest 100 tests; the export has them all") with Export CSV at its right. `E` exports while focus is on the board, but not from a card's menu, the drawer or a text field. With no tests it is locked with "Nothing to export". Preparing, over-cap and failure with Retry are Discover's states. |

**Clicks:** exporting the Testing Board takes **1** (Export CSV), or **0** with `E` while a card has focus. Before, there was no export. By keyboard from the page title it is Tab, then Enter. The Screener has no page yet (WF-1b), so it has no button.

**Tables with Export CSV:** 1 → **2** in the UI (Discover, Testing Board). Endpoints with `?format=csv`: 1 → **3**.
