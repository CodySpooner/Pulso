## What was built

1. **Migration 0010** (`packages/core/src/pulso_core/migrations/versions/0010_workspace_product_economics.py`) adds:
   - **SQL `landed_cost()`**, the Python function line by line, with three helpers:
     - `half_even()`: exact numeric `div`/`mod`, Python's `round()` on a Fraction;
     - `usd_line()`: a line's USD cents or its missing code, with the currency exponent from `currencies` and BIGINT checks before and after converting;
     - `usd_rate()`: the rate's own date with a 4-day look-back, the blend before a manual rate.

     It has the same lines, BIGINT checks, `MISSING` order, R-16 nulling and errors (unknown code, unknown currency, price ≤ 0).
   - **The view `workspace_product_economics`**: one row per workspace (`settings` row) and non-merged product. It equals `calculate()` with no overrides:
     - quantity 1, no DDP, Settings' duty, fees, returns and CPA;
     - the lower median of live USD store prices;
     - each live supplier listing's latest snapshot;
     - the cheapest exact US quote at q = 1 on the variant's newest capture day (DEC-258);
     - `best()` across listings.

     Its columns are every line, `complete`, `missing[]`, `sources` (jsonb, per input) and `as_of` (the oldest observed input). Freight is US only (`settings.default_country`, pinned by `v1_market`).
   - **Downgrade** drops the view and the four functions. `test_schema_integration.py` runs head → 0001 → head → base → head. A function left behind would fail the second `CREATE FUNCTION`.
2. **Model wiring:** `pulso_core.models.workspace_product_economics` is a `Table` on its own `MetaData`, so autogenerate never tries to create it.
3. **Golden vectors:** `packages/core/tests/fixtures/landed_cost_vectors.json` has **30 vectors, 27 of them through the view**, generated from `landed_cost()` and spot-checked by hand:
   - the worked example 1503 / 5012 bps, and a loss of −339 / −3393;
   - half-even ties 2.5 → 2 and 1.5 → 2;
   - JPY 667 (duty 133), KWD 411, and CNY 0 and 2;
   - DDP, and JPY at q = 3 with a per-order customs fee;
   - duty not set and duty 0; each gap alone and several together in `MISSING` order;
   - owner estimates;
   - BIGINT: payment, landed cost, JPY freight at a 1e-8 rate, `margin_bps`, net (twice, one with `net_bps` still in range), and p2-12's JPY 2^62 at q = 2;
   - an FX rate of 0.

   The 3 function-only vectors need an input the default scenario lacks: q > 1 or DDP.
4. **Parity tests** (`packages/core/tests/test_landed_cost_vectors.py`):
   - Python against every vector.
   - The SQL twin against every vector.
   - The view against the 27 view vectors. Each vector is seeded in a savepoint, and every field is checked against the vector, against `calculate()` on the same rows (supplier, method, sources) and `as_of`.
   - Two workspaces with different settings (1503 vs 1770); a merged product has no row.
   - **The view = endpoint hook:** in `apps/api/tests/test_profit.py`, every bare `{product_id}` calc (the default scenario) also asserts that the view's row equals the endpoint's answer, sources and `as_of` included. That covers every existing default-scenario profit test: XR-16, XR-23, re-parse, FX date, variants, the unquoted supplier.
5. **Discover's best margin** (`apps/api/src/pulso_api/routers/products.py`). `GET /v1/products` reads the view for `principal.workspace_id` and the page's ids, and `best_margin_minor` is `Margin | Incomplete`:
   - `Margin`: a `Sourced` with `margin_bps` and `supplier_listing_id`. Its as-of and source are the oldest observed input's (DEC-259 e), and it is stale if any input is.
   - `Incomplete`: a `NoSource` with `missing[]` and "Needs a freight quote." style reasons, or "No workspace settings yet."
   - `openapi.json` and `schema.d.ts` were regenerated with `pnpm gen:api`.
6. **The web cell** (display only), in `apps/web/src/app/(app)/discover/feed-views.tsx` (grid and table):

## Margin sort and filter: not cheap to index (EXPLAIN)

On a scratch database migrated through this migration in my container (then numbered 0009; the SQL is unchanged), with 5,000 products, one workspace and no listings (a floor):
- **A Discover page** (`product_id = ANY(48 ids)`, the router's query): the ids reach a `pk_products` bitmap index scan, so only the page's products are costed. **5.9 ms**.
- **A margin sort** (`ORDER BY margin_minor DESC NULLS LAST LIMIT 48`): a seq scan on `products` costs all 5,000 rows before sorting. **183 ms**, and that is with nothing to cost. It grows with products times listings.

No index can cover a view computed through plpgsql functions. A margin sort or `margin_min_bp` filter needs a materialized per-workspace table. Backlog row 1 below, DEC-265 (e). The scratch database was dropped afterwards.
