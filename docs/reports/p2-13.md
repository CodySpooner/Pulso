## Chosen values (DEC-249, all starting guesses)

- **Weights:** image cosine 0.55, title cosine 0.30, pHash similarity 0.15. The vault's `title_trgm` 0.10 is folded into the title weight (no trigram feature in this task). The score is a weighted mean over the components present, so a missing component has weight 0 and the rest renormalise (DEC-040). Components are rounded to `numeric(5,4)` first, then the score is rounded to 4 places (half-even) and clamped to [0, 1]. A stored row re-scores to its stored score, and the boundaries compare exactly.
- **Thresholds:** auto 0.90, queue 0.75, top-k 20.
- **Price band:** retail ÷ supplier cost in [1.2, 8], both converted to USD cents with `pulso_core.landed_cost.to_usd` at the stored `fx_rates` rate as of the snapshot's `observed_for`. Two retail prices (or two costs) may differ by at most 8 ÷ 1.2 ≈ 6.67×, the widest spread two markups of one cost can have. A missing price, a missing rate or a non-positive price gives `price_sane` NULL. An insane or unknown price never auto-links but may queue: it is a gate, not the vault's score cap.
- **Version:** `scorer_version` is `m3-` plus the first 12 hex of a sha256 over every config value. A test changes each field in turn and asserts a new version.
- **Link-direction rule:** only listings without a product move. One product-less listing joins the other listing's canonical product (via `merged_into_id`). Two product-less listings form a new product, named after the older listing (`first_seen_at`, then id) with its `first_seen_at`. Two listings already on different products stay `pending`, because joining them is a product merge, which belongs to M-5 and the queue. The algorithm therefore never empties or splits a product. I chose this over "the listing with fewer observations moves": that rule moves listings off established products and can leave an empty product behind.
- **Manual constraints:** decided candidates (`accepted`, `rejected`, `merged`, `split`) are never touched. A listing whose latest decision has a non-`algo` actor never moves. A listing never joins a product that holds the other side of one of its rejected or split pairs, so a rejected pair can't be linked through a third listing. Both listings are locked `FOR UPDATE` before the decision.

## Acceptance

- **(a) Threshold tests on fixed vectors:** `test_route_thresholds` covers exactly 0.9000 and 0.7500 and one step below each (0.8999, 0.7499), plus insane and unknown prices at 0.99 that only queue. `test_fixed_vectors_route_end_to_end` runs component vectors through `combined()` and `route()`. `test_combined_score_renormalises_over_present_components` covers a missing title (renormalised to 0.9029), image only, and clamping. `test_cross_currency_prices_compare_in_usd_never_raw_minor_units` uses JPY (exponent 0) and KWD (exponent 3) prices that raw minor units would misjudge both ways.
- **(b) R-15:** `test_r15_every_pointer_change_has_exactly_one_decision_and_reruns_add_nothing` builds a 21-listing universe (6 planes of supplier + retail + queue-band listings, one listing joining an existing product, one without an embedding). It asserts that every listing whose `product_id` changed has exactly one new decision whose from/to equal the old and new pointer, and every unchanged listing has none.
- **(c) Rejected pair and manual decision survive:** `test_pending_rows_rescore_and_decided_rows_never_change` (the rejected row is byte-identical after the run, and a bridging listing can't join the rejected pair) and `test_manual_decisions_are_never_undone` (an owner `unlink` and an owner `link` survive two runs, and no decision is added).
- **(d) A second run writes nothing:** the R-15 test compares every `match_candidates` and `match_decisions` row, and every listing pointer, before and after a second run.
- **(e) Flag off:** `test_flag_off_logs_and_never_opens_the_database` (unset and `off`): the task logs `matcher_disabled` and `get_session` is never called.
- **HNSW path:** `test_neighbours_use_hnsw_and_exclude_same_store_and_same_product` EXPLAINs the generation query with `enable_seqscan = off` and finds `Index Scan using ix_images_embedding`. It also checks that same-store, same-product and merged-into-same-product listings are excluded.

## Observations (not changed)

- **The matcher has no work on real data yet.** Every real `images.embedding` and `listings.title_embedding` is NULL until M-2 part b (OpenCLIP) runs, so a flag-on run today only counts `skipped_no_embedding`.
- **No production code creates products or sets `listings.product_id` today.** Collectors write listings with `product_id` NULL. The matcher creates a product only when it links a pair; a listing with no match stays product-less. The vault's step 5 sentence ("a listing with no link above 0.90 and no queued candidate becomes a new product") is not built. It needs a decision on where singleton products come from, and Discover only lists products.
- **FX lookup:** the worker passes `pulso_sources.fx_frankfurter.rate_as_of` (D4's capture cutoff, unbounded carry-forward), because `pulso-sources` already depends on `pulso-ml`. p2-10's loader (DEC-246 d) uses the rate's own date within 4 days instead. Both are fine for a 1.2–8× sanity band. One shared lookup in `pulso_core` would let the matcher drop the injected callable.
- **No `ml` consumer or schedule:** the collector compose runs `-Q collect,default` and an `ops` worker; nothing consumes `ml`. `ml.match` has no Beat entry, so nothing piles up in Redis. Infra is outside this task's ownership.
- **Transitive constraints are partial:** the matcher won't join a listing to a product holding the other side of its rejected pair. It doesn't model longer chains, and a later manual merge is M-5's job.
- **Full pass per run:** one HNSW query per embedded listing. Restricting runs to new or changed listings is a later optimisation (`ponytail:` note in `match()`).
