## Behaviour

`family_velocity(now, week_ago, days_observed)` reads one product's rows in **one** family:
- `now` and `week_ago` are its rows for t and t−7, or None when there's no row;
- `days_observed` is the days since its first row in that family, t included. That's the same input `lifecycle_stage()` takes, so SC-6 computes it once for both.

**Values:**
- V7 and G7 are the stored values. DEC-275 d already computes G7 = ln((V7(t)+5)/(V7(t−7)+5)) over the stable listing set (covered at t−14 and t), so no G7 maths was re-implemented.
- Acc(t) = G7(t) − G7(t−7) subtracts the two rows' stored G7, so no column is needed (as p4-01's report said).
- `Row` is a protocol. A `Feature`, the `ProductDailyFeature` ORM row and a `Demand` all satisfy it; I checked the first two under mypy with a temporary file, then deleted it.

**Every NULL says why (DEC-310 b):**

| Reason | When | Window named |
|---|---|---|
| `no source` | No row at t. That covers `shp_bump` (no feature code yet) and products with no member listing (DEC-275 e) | — |
| `insufficient history` | Fewer than 8 days (V7), 15 (G7) or 22 (Acc) | — |
| `gap <window>` | History long enough, value NULL | V7 `t-7..t` · G7 `t-14..t` · Acc: the NULL G7's window, `t-14..t` or `t-21..t-7` (G7(t−7) NULL or its row missing) |

**Gap rules (DEC-310 c):**
- The stored rows already apply the vault's tolerance over their full windows. A gap over 3 days, or an uncovered window end, is NULL. A gap of 3 days or less keeps the exact value, because inventory differencing spans it, and SC-1 reports it as `has_gap` True.
- Only the rows at t and t−7 are read, so nothing is interpolated or carried forward from a neighbouring day.
- DEC-275 also sets `has_gap` when V7 is NULL only because history is short. SC-1 doesn't count that as a gap, so a new product reads `insufficient history` with `has_gap` False.

**How this differs from DEC-307 (Lifecycle):**
- Lifecycle gives no stage on any gap flag. SC-1 keeps an exact value through a tolerated gap and flags it. Velocity is data that Score Definitions §3 carries with `gap` beside a present value. Being strict would blank G7 for about two weeks and Acc for three after a one-day outage, and Trend Score needs G7.
- Lifecycle's "NULL without a flag means short history" would call two real gaps "history". In both, neither row is flagged: a 4-day gap in t−21..t−15, and the second of two listings missing exactly t−7.

**Acceptance, case by case:**
- **Gappy fixtures (10 cases in one table):**
  - tolerated 3-day gaps in t−7..t and in t−14..t−8 keep exact values and set the flag;
  - 4-day gaps in each of the three weeks are NULL with the window named;
  - uncovered window ends at t, t−7, t−14 and t−21 are NULL with the window named.
- **No interpolation:** a missing t−7 row gives Acc `gap t-21..t-7` at 22 days and `insufficient history` at 21. No neighbouring row is read.
- **The 3 → 30 jump:** V7 30. G7 = ln(35/8) = 1.476, not +900%. Acc = ln(35/8) − ln(8/6).
- **A product with no history:** 1 day reads `insufficient history` × 3 with `has_gap` False. At 10 days, the t−7 row's DEC-275 short-history flag isn't counted as a gap.
- **A family with no source yet:** all three are `no source`, never 0 (DEC-040).
- **History boundaries:** 7/8 days (V7), 14/15 (G7) and 21/22 (Acc).
- **The vault lamp:** Acc 0.368 − 0.160 = 0.208, plus a negative acceleration.
- **The stable panel:** a listing joining mid-window moves neither G7 nor Acc. An emptied panel reads as a gap (the pinned limit). The second of two listings missing exactly t−7 reads as a gap even though neither row is flagged.
- **The version:** a `revision` bump and a `FEATURE_VERSION` change each give a new version.
