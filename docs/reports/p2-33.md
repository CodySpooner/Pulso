## Chosen rules (DEC-272)

- **One report at a time.** Google ranks per category, so lists don't mix categories. The default is the newest imported week and that week's first category (alphabetical). Changing the week keeps the category. If a report was downloaded twice, only its newest capture shows.
- **Major brand:** 10 or more rows under one brand (case-insensitive) in the same week and category. Why: the owner wants dropshippable items, and Google's list is brand-heavy (in the owner's Apparel sample, the top 200 brands are 76% of rows). A white-label item rarely sits beside nine more ranked products under one brand. Rows with no brand are never flagged. The threshold is the named constant `MAJOR_BRAND_ROWS` and is computed on read, so tuning it needs no migration.
- **Weekly change:** `value` = previous rank − rank, so it is positive when the product moved up. A product absent last week is "New" in words, never 0. If no earlier week is imported, the change is "—" with "no source yet", and a line names the week to import.
- **Stale:** the newest imported week started more than 14 days ago (Google's own lag is up to 2 weeks). Browsing an earlier week on purpose isn't stale.
- **Internal use:** a line on the page states Google's terms. The page is behind sign-in like every app route, and no public page shows these rows (DEC-267 f).
