## Keypresses and clicks per decision

- **Keyboard (the verify):** **1 keypress per decision.** Playwright decided 200 fixture pairs (150 A, 50 R), waiting for each pair to render before its key, in **19.9 s of machine time**. 30 minutes for 200 pairs leaves 9 s of looking per pair, and input never waits on the network or an animation: the next pair is selected before the request returns.
- **Mouse:** 1 click per accept or reject (decision bar). Merge takes 2 clicks (Merge, then "Merge n listings"). Split takes 2 clicks plus one tick per listing (Split, ticks, "Split n listings off").
- **Keyboard merge and split:** M, Tab, Tab, Enter (focus opens on "Swap direction", so a stray Enter never merges). X, Space per listing, Tab to "Split n listings off", Enter.
- **Undo:** U or Ctrl+Z, 1 key. J/K move, S skips, 1–4 pick the gallery image.
