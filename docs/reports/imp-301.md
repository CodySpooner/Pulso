## Cause

`apps/api/tests/test_products.py::test_feed_items_trace_every_number` expected the Steady card's `as_of` to be its CJ snapshot, `NOW - 9 h`. The card's `as_of` is the oldest `as_of` of its feeding families (`products.py` `item`: `min(s.as_of for s in fed)`). The velocity family's `as_of` is `min(feature_date + 1 day at 00:00Z, computed_at)` (`velocity_stat`, DEC-031). The fixture dates that feature yesterday, so its `as_of` is today 00:00Z (`computed_at` is the insert time, always later). From 09:00Z on, `NOW - 9 h` is later than 00:00Z, so the velocity family is the oldest and the card's `as_of` is 00:00Z. CI ran at 09:00:29Z and got exactly `2026-10-25 00:00Z`.

Reproduced on the real clock (09:21Z, 1 failed). With `NOW` pinned, main's test passed at 00:30Z and 08:59Z and failed at 09:01Z and 23:59Z. The production rule is right; the test assumed a time of day.

## Fix

`apps/api/tests/test_products.py`, one assertion became two:
- the CJ family's own `as_of` is `NOW - 9 h` (`supplier_count.as_of`), so the expectation is pinned to a family;
- the card's `as_of` is `min(NOW - 9 h, day_end)`: the oldest of the CJ snapshot and the velocity family's day end. The stores family (`NOW - 6 h`) is never the oldest.

## Leftovers (not fixed here; candidate backlog rows)

1. **`test_overview_traces_each_stat` and midnight.** It expects `age_days == 9` from `first_seen_at = NOW - 9 days`, where `NOW` is set at import and the server uses its own date. If a run imports before 00:00Z and reaches this test after it, the answer is 10. The window is the minute or two between collection and that test. A fix: expect `(datetime.now(UTC).date() - (NOW - timedelta(days=9)).date()).days`, or seed `first_seen_at` from a per-test clock.
2. **`body.as_of > NOW - 5 min` in the fixed test.** The newest raw payload is inserted when the fixture runs, but `NOW` is set at import. Locally, apps/api's 16 files take 3:00 and `test_products.py` is the 11th, so the margin is about 3 minutes. A slower runner, or more apps/api tests before it, would turn this red. A fix: compare with a timestamp taken inside the test.
