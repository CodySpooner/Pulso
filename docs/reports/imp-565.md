Claude · branch `CodySpooner/imp-565-cj-stock-readers` · DEC-356 (proposed (imp-565)) · source: DEC-348's Split paragraph and `docs/reports/imp-545.md` § Part b · no migration, endpoint, flag, dependency, writer or UI change.

## Outcome

Every reader on part b's list now reads price and stock separately, through part a's `PRICE_CAPTURE` and `STOCK_READ` (`pulso_core.landed_cost_loader`). No copy of either predicate was written. Suppose a CJ listing's newest row is a stock-only row:

- **Suppliers tab:** the unit cost stays on the older price row. Stock comes from the stock row, and the stock Stat carries that row's `captured_at` and `raw_payload_id`.
- **Overview:** the best in-stock supplier offer keeps the price row's cost, and a stock-out removes the offer.
- **Discover:** the list's supplier stat traces to the price row.
- **Matching:** match review and the matcher's price fact read the price capture.
- **Pulse:** an unknown total supersedes the day before's stock-out.

A listing whose newest row carries both price and stock reads exactly as before.

## Decisions (DEC-356)

- **One lateral helper, part a's predicates.** `newest_snapshot()` lives in `products.py`, and `product_suppliers.py` already imports from there. The matcher and match review add the filter in place, because `pulso_ml` can't import `pulso_api`.
- **Suppliers tab:**
  - Rating and orders stay on the price row, since a CJ stock row carries neither. `as_of` is the price row's.
  - `in_stock` and `stock` come from the stock reading.
- **Discover's listing row is its price capture.**
  - `captured_at` and `raw_payload_id` are the price row's, so `supplier_count` and the Overview's appearance counts trace to the newest price capture, not to a newer stock capture.
  - The price lateral is an inner join. A listing whose only rows are stock captures would drop out, but no writer makes one: `CJAdapter.upsert` creates the listing with its price row, and a stock capture needs a known variant.
- **Pulse:** `STOCK_READ` in both stock laterals. A stock-out followed by an unknown total now lists the offer as flipped back to "not known out of stock" (DEC-243), as R-23 re-arms in part a.
- **Left as is:**
  - `list_products`' aggregate price lateral (Discover's price filters and sorts) reads store listings only (`usd_store`), and a stock capture never writes a store listing.
  - `product_sellers.py` and the Shopify adapter read store listings only.
  - Screener `best_rated()` is in the backlog rows below.
- **Also changed:** on these surfaces, a newest price-only row with stock null no longer hides an older stock reading. That is DEC-348 (d), which `stocked()` already follows. It applies to any source, not only CJ.
