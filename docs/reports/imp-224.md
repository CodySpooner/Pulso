## Choices (DEC-260)

- **(c) `rejected` for a below-band pending pair.** The brief asked for an existing status. `rejected` is the only one that means "not one product"; `accepted` and `merged` say the opposite. The costs are recorded:
  - it is never re-scored, like every decided row;
  - the GTIN pass skips it;
  - DEC-249 (h)'s third-listing veto applies, which fails closed (that link queues instead).
  - UX-1 can tell it from an owner reject, because an owner reject always writes `reject` decision rows (DEC-257 a).
- **(d) A sweep, not a hook in `link()`.** A pair can end up on one product through an owner accept, a split `into` or a GTIN link too. One query over the pending rows covers every path.
- **(e) No per-listing rollback.** The review's optional broad catch was left out; the two known faults are guarded.
