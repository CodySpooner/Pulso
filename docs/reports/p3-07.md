**The endpoint** is scoped exactly like `/v1/amazon/ranks`: it needs `current_principal` (401 without a session) and applies no workspace filter, because `amazon_ranks` and products are global market data.

- It reads the rows of every day's newest capture that carry GTINs and links them with `link_products()`.
- For each list (category × `bsr`/`movers`) it keeps this product's newest ranked day. When two ASINs link to the product, the better rank wins.
- Each row is the `AmazonRankRow` that `/amazon` uses: rank, `as_of` (observed_for), `captured_at`, `raw_payload_id`, ASIN, title and price.
- The answer also carries:
  - `source`/`source_name` (null without the source row);
  - `imported` (whether any list was imported);
  - the rows' newest `as_of` and `captured_at`;
  - `stale` (the same rule as `/v1/amazon/ranks`: the newest day is over 14 days old).
- An unknown id answers 404. A merged id answers 409 (`live_product`, like the other product sub-resources).

**Defaults recorded in DEC-293:**

- A list the product dropped out of shows its last ranked day, dated. It is never carried forward.
- When two ASINs share the product's GTIN, the best rank stands for the product.
- "No source yet" covers both a missing source row and no import. The source's `enabled` flag isn't read, because Amazon is CSV-only.
- The section sits after Search keywords. Most products have no Amazon row, so placing it there doesn't push the keyword actions down.
