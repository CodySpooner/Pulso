### Why margin, not padding

An outline is drawn outside the border box, and padding is inside it. Padding therefore moves the ring *toward* the neighbours, not away from them. Only space outside the link (margin) or a tighter offset can move the ring off the next word.

The budget per side is fixed: the space beside the link plus the added margin has to hold the inner air, the 2 px stroke and the outer air.
- **Left side:** the space is 3.16 px at 13 px Geist. A 1 px offset plus 2 px of margin leaves 2.16 px to "the".
- **Right side:** the "." has no space before it, so it needs 4 px of margin for 1 px of air. The arrow trim gives back 2 px of that, so at rest the arrow-to-"." distance grows by only 2 px.

### Rejected along the way (rendered and compared, `scratchpad` crops only)

- **0 px offset, no margin.** No layout change, but the ring hugs the "C", and the "." still overlaps the ring by 2 px.
- **2 px symmetric margin, no end margin.** "the" clears, but the "." touches the ring.
- **3 px symmetric margin.** Both sides clear, but at rest it reads as a double space before the link.
- **A negative margin on the arrow instead of the viewBox trim.** Chromium draws the outline around the overflowing icon, which leaves a broken notch on the ring's right edge.
