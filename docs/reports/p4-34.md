## Behaviour

- **What's read:** each watched product's newest row of the rule's kind dated before `as_of`'s UTC day. A day *d* row holds captures before *d*+1 00:00Z and is written after it, so the nightly call at the cutoff reads day *d*.
- **`velocity_spike`** (`pct` 50, `window_days` 7, `min_volume` 10, defaults that are guesses recorded in DEC-353 c):
  - It reads the Lifecycle row's V7(t) and V7(t−7) in the primary family.
  - It fires when (V7(t) − V7(t−7)) / max(V7(t−7), 1) ≥ `pct` and V7(t) ≥ `min_volume`, and re-arms when the rise is under 95% of `pct`. A fall never fires.
  - **Gap-suppressed:** the night is skipped when either week holds a gap: a collection gap of the family's source detected before `as_of` overlapping t−14..t+1, or a kept value's `gap` flag. The rule reports `skipped` with `{reason: gap, from, to}`, re-runs included, no alert is written, and the product's state doesn't move. The next gap-free night is compared as usual.
- **`saturation_cross`** (`threshold` 0–100, `direction` above or below, both required): fires once per crossing of the stored Saturation value, and re-arms past 5% of the line on the other side.
- **Both:**
  - the first row seen is the reference;
  - a NULL row, a Lifecycle row without V7(t−7), or no row is `no_source` and keeps the last state, never a crossing from 0;
  - a new model_version is a new reference;
  - dedupe `rule_id:product:<id>:<row date>`;
  - the payload carries old, new (with cutoff, source, model_version), threshold, as_of, link and the product's name.
- **API:**
  - each type takes only its own params: another type's field or a missing required one is a 422 on that field, and defaults fill the rest;
  - score rules take a product or a watchlist, supplier rules a product or an offer (422 otherwise);
  - another workspace's watchlist is a 404.
- **Builder:**
  - "When [this product / any product on Pet] [velocity rises by] [50] % over the prior week, with at least [10] units /7d";
  - "When [this product / any product on Pet] [Saturation crosses] [above / below] [65]".
  - The Rules list shows the threshold and the target ("Pet · every product on it" links `/watchlists?list=…`), and a velocity rule's gap skip as "1 evaluation skipped: data gap Oct 8 → Oct 22".
