## What was built

`packages/ml/src/pulso_ml/features/`:

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| `repository.py` | The only table door. `AsOf(cutoff)` provides `membership()` (replays `match_decisions` before the cutoff through DEC-257's `membership`), `levels(family)` (stock captures with `captured_at < cutoff`, the newest parse of each capture) and `covered(family)` (UTC days whose target succeeded, dated by the payload's capture time). It also holds `FAMILIES` (`shp_inv`, `cj_stock`), the `Feature` row and `write()`, which is `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING … RETURNING`. |
| `demand.py` | Pure maths: `daily_units`, `known_days`, `parse_count` / `cumulative_units`, `velocity` (V7) and `product_demand` (V7, G7, coverage, has_gap). `CONFIG` and `FEATURE_VERSION` = `001-0754a5e6a014`. |
| `daily.py` | `cutoff(d)` = d+1 00:00Z; `compute(session, d)` returns sorted `Feature` rows; `run(session, d)` writes them and returns how many were new, and the caller commits. |

Definitions (DEC-275 b–e, vault [[Velocity & Acceleration]] "As built"):
- **Units.** A capture's units are its variants' drops since their previous capture; a rise is a restock and adds 0. An unknown level breaks the chain, and a covered day only counts while every variant's latest level is known, so a vanished field or an overselling Shopify variant reads as unknown, not 0.
- **V7(t).** Sums days t−6..t, from a covered baseline t−7 to a covered t. No coverage run longer than 3 days is allowed, and one failing stable listing makes the product NULL.
- **G7.** ln((V7+5)/(V7prev+5)), over listings covered at t−14 and t. V7 itself uses listings covered at t−7 and t.
- **coverage** is the share of member-listing days observed over t−7..t.
- **has_gap** is set when V7 is NULL or a stable listing missed a day.
- **Rows.** One per (product, day, family, version), only for products with a member listing that family has seen a known level for. With none there's no row, so Discover's "no source" stays.

## Tests

`packages/ml/tests/test_demand.py` (pure, 13 tests) covers:
- `daily_units`: a restock in the interval plus variants summed into the listing; an unknown level breaking the chain; an unknown level making a covered day uncovered.
- Cumulative counts: a negative cumulative delta (0, `negative_delta`); "1.2K" / "15K" / "2.25M+" / "1,234" parsing, with "1.2K" → "1.2K" and "1.2K" → "1.25K" below precision.
- Exclusions: an implausible drop of 400 against a median of 2 (excluded and flagged, while 25 units counts); a store-wide sync (5 of 5 in one crawl excluded, 2 of 5 and 3 of 5 counted, and the rule off for CJ).
- V7: the 7-day sum; unchanged/304 days staying covered; 3 missing days tolerated but not 4; both window ends required.
- G7: `ln(26/12)` with the prior of 5; a listing added mid-window leaving G7 unchanged.

`packages/ml/tests/test_leakage.py` (Postgres) uses a 45-day fixture of one Shopify store (6 listings) and CJ (4 listings, one with 2 variants) on 7 products. It holds a restock, an implausible drop, a day-15 store-wide sync, and a late snapshot (`observed_for` 2 days before `captured_at`). It also holds a misread parse superseded by a re-parse, and CJ and Shopify missing day 30. Shopify is out for days 30–34. A CJ target fails on day 33, and change-only snapshots leave unchanged days. One listing oversells from day 1 and another from day 36. Day 41's CJ run is planned at 23:59:30 and fetched at 00:00:00 the next day, and C4 is first captured exactly at day 41's cutoff. The day-40 merge (P6 into P5) and split (L2 onto P7) are stamped exactly at day 39's cutoff.
- `test_features_equal_those_from_a_copy_truncated_at_the_cutoff`: for days 15, 20, 29, 33, 36, 39, 40, 41 and 45, `compute()` on the full DB equals `compute()` on a copy truncated inside a savepoint that is rolled back afterwards. In the copy, decisions, targets' late payloads, snapshots and payloads at or after the cutoff are gone, with the append-only trigger disabled inside that savepoint. Today's `listings.product_id` and `merged_into_id` are also cleared. Equality is exact, not 1e-9.
- `test_membership_follows_the_decisions_known_at_the_cutoff`:
  - day 20: P1's Shopify V7 is 6 + 4, with day 15's sync excluded;
  - day 20: P1's CJ V7 is 6 × 3 + 4, with the restock adding 0 and the misread superseded;
  - day 39: P6 has a row and P7 doesn't;
  - day 45: P6 has no row, P1 = 7, P7 = 3, P1's G7 is NULL (t−14 fell in the outage), the overselling P2 reads `(None, None, 0, True)`, and P4 has coverage 1 (L6 untracked);
  - day 45: P5's CJ row has a G7, has_gap and coverage 0.75 (C4, new, waits for its baseline).
- `test_rerunning_a_day_writes_nothing_and_a_new_version_appends` (day 33):

  - a re-run inserts 0 and every xmin is unchanged;

## Leftovers and ceilings

- **Shopify coverage** is per store (`shopify_variants_js` targets the store), so a listing outside that day's `.js` panel reads as covered. Per-listing evidence would fix it if `shp_inv` ever runs again (DEC-261).
- **Sync after an outage:** the first crawl after a gap shows most listings dropping, which can read as a sync. This is part of the Phase 4 tuning of the 60% / 5 guesses, together with the implausible thresholds.
- **`AsOf.levels`** reads each family's whole history per day; bound it if a long backfill measures slow.
- **Parser rule:** the capture predicates repeat the source writers' (DEC-120), because `pulso_ml` doesn't import `pulso_sources`. If a writer's request shape changes, `FAMILIES` must follow.
- **Re-parse ceiling:** a re-parse of an old capture after its day was computed doesn't change the stored row (append-only), but a later backfill of that day would read the new parse. Bump `revision` when a parser fix should flow into features.
