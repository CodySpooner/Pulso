## What was built

- **API (additive):**
  - `landed_cost()` takes `ad_cost_minor` and returns `ad_cost_minor`, `net_minor` and `net_bps`, which are null unless the row is complete and BIGINT-bounded.
  - The calc request takes an optional `ad_cost_minor` (an owner estimate). When it's omitted, the endpoint uses `settings.default_cpa_minor`.
  - The calc answer echoes the rates the lines used (duty, payment fee %, fixed fee, Shopify fee, returns) and the chosen quote's `freight_min_days`/`freight_max_days`.
  - Every `sources` entry gains `source_name` and `stale` (DEC-238's rule).
- **Profit tab** (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/products/[id]/profit/`):
  - **Results:** three tiles: profit per order and margin (both after ad cost, with sign and ▲/▼), and break-even ad cost.
  - **Scenario panel:** sell price (an input plus a slider from about half to double the market price), units per order 1–4, returns %, payment fee %, ad cost per order, and Reset to defaults.
  - **Per-order breakdown:** every line, % of price, and the source with its age. Every amount opens its provenance by hover, focus or tap: source, as-of and raw payload for an observation; the formula for a total.
  - **Recompute:** only changed inputs are sent, debounced 120 ms. The result shown is always the server's: the old result stays dimmed under a 2 px hairline until the new one arrives, and a failed recompute keeps the last result with Retry.
- **R-16 and states:**
  - **Incomplete:** a banner lists each missing input in plain words, with a Settings link when duty isn't set. The results read "Withheld" with what they need, and the lines show amber gaps ("Not quoted", "Not set", "Enter a price").
  - **No supplier listing:** "no source yet", with the CSV import link.
  - **No market price:** the input asks for one. A typed price prices the order and anchors the slider.
  - **Stale inputs:** a note names each source and its last capture, and each line's source tag turns amber with "stale".
  - **Loading** is a skeleton; a **first-load error** shows the panel error with Retry.
  - Owner estimates carry a "Your estimate" chip.
- **Settings defaults:** the tab doesn't read `GET /v1/settings`. Its defaults are the calc answer for the unchanged scenario, which already applies DEC-251's duty, returns and import fee. A Settings change shows up on the next calc with no tab change (DEC-256 c).
- **DEC-261:** with the store crawler off for good, a missing price now reads "Pulso has no market price for it yet. Enter your price to see profit." It no longer implies a store crawl is coming.
