**Acceptance, item by item:**
- **Top 3:** they pick the largest abs(contribution), a negative one included, with points = 50/3 × contribution. Opportunity's are as given (Vitest). On the card they read "▲ Velocity growth (Shopify inventory) +15.3 points", "+5.5" and "+4.7" (Playwright).
- **Penalties:** each renders as its own step after the reasons: "▼ Saturation penalty −7.0 points", and Opportunity's "Margin below your floor −15.0". A missing penalty shows its wording and no number.
- **NULL row, no row and `ads_growth`:**
  - a NULL row reads "Insufficient signal · {reason}" and keeps its day as its as-of;
  - no row reads "No source yet", with "Scores run nightly; none has scored this product yet.";
  - `ads_growth` reads "No source yet: Ad-count growth".
  - All are covered in Vitest and Playwright, for both the card and the Discover cell.
- **Duty not set and Margin below your floor:** the first comes from `cautions` (an amber chip linking to `/settings#duty`). The second needs `reason` plus the `margin_floor` penalty (a red chip, plus its step).
- **ff_scores on:** four cards, each with its as-of. A reason's source and the row's cutoff open on keyboard focus. Tab reaches "How Trend Score works" with a solid focus ring, and Enter opens `/methodology#trend-score`.
- **ff_scores off:** no `/scores` request, and the key stats are the same five.
- **Discover:** a row's Trend cell opens `/products/{id}#score-trend`, and the card is focused and in view. The table's Trend header links to `/methodology#trend-score`.
