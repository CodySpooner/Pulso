## Acceptance, item by item

- **Freshness 1.0 / 0.5 / missing at the exact 1.5× and 3× boundaries, daily and weekly:** `test_freshness_is_1_to_1_5_cadences_then_0_5_to_3_then_stale[cj|google_trends]`. It checks 1 at 0 and at exactly 1.5×, 0.5 at 1.5× + 1 µs and at exactly 3×, and stale (missing, `as_of` kept) at 3× + 1 µs.
- **Confidence equals the hand calculation for the lamp (ads missing) with one stale component:**
  - Contract fixture (0.4 / 0.2 / 0.4) with a 12-day-old slope: (0.4 × 1 + 0.2 × 0.5) / 1 = **0.5**, with coverage 0.6 and the value unchanged.
  - The vault lamp under Trend's real config: fresh, **0.85**. With a 12-day-old slope, 0.40 + 0.20 + 0.25 × 0.5 = **0.725**, and the value is still 68.6.
- **A component beyond 3× is renormalised away and named in `missing`:** in the contract test the slope is missing and stale with weight_eff 0; `missing` is `("gt_slope", "ads_growth")`, the value is cj alone, and coverage = confidence = 0.4. For the real lamp with the slope 21 days + 1 s old: missing, weights 0.40/0.60 and 0.20/0.60, confidence 0.6, and **Trend 71.4 instead of 68.6**.
- **Old stored explanations without the new fields still validate:** `test_rows_stored_before_freshness_still_validate` drops `confidence`, `cautions` and the three component fields and validates the result. p4-20's Opportunity shape (`confidence` 0.7, `cautions` `["duty_not_set"]`) still validates too.
- **Opportunity's confidence drops with a stale Trend row and names the caution:** a 2-day-old Trend row gives freshness 0.5, confidence 0.7 (0.9 fresh), value 65.7 and `stale_input`. A row 3 days + 1 s old is missing and stale: Opportunity is 63.4, confidence 0.5, and its `as_of` is no longer the old row's. With duty also not set: confidence 0.3 and cautions `stale_input`, `duty_not_set`.
- **Did the stale rule change a stored value, and so the version?** Yes for **Trend**: a search slope captured more than 21 days before the cutoff is now missing. Revision 1 (`000-8236875f835c` → `001-300dc06245a2`), so new nights append under the new model_version and old rows stay. **Saturation** and **Lifecycle** can't move, because their inputs are as of the cutoff, so their versions are unchanged. **Opportunity** went `000-de3f6e76320d` → `001-b2dd5054317c`; it is read-time and never stored.
- **Mutation check:** a hand mutation run of 23 mutants over `fresh`, `confidence`, `explain`, `CADENCE` and the thresholds, the Component validator, Opportunity's cautions and confidence, the Trend and Saturation stamping, and Lifecycle's freshness. **23 of 23 were killed** (each makes at least one test fail). The script is not committed.
- **Methodology drift test with the new `#confidence`:** `test_the_committed_export_is_the_code` passes. `test_confidence_states_the_formula_the_cadences_and_the_stale_rule` checks the formula, the cadence days, the score-row flags, the thresholds, and that the rule list's wording follows the constants.
- **Vitest for the section:** `#confidence` renders the formula, `stale:` as a rule name, cj 36 h / 3 d, google_trends weekly 10.5 d / 21 d, and the score-row note, with no "Not built yet". An empty cadence list shows its line and no table. `every()` and `span()` are tested too.
- **The new fields reach the schema:** `pnpm gen:api` adds `freshness`, `stale` and `freshness_reason` to `Component`. `Explanation.confidence` and `cautions` were already there.
