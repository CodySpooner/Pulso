**Feed.**
- Filters: category (exact id), price min and max (USD, overlap with the band), source, first seen within 7, 30 or 90 days, and has stores.
- Sorts: momentum (default), newest, fewest stores.
- Paging: a keyset cursor (the `[key, id]` JSON string), `limit` 48 by default, max 100. Merged products never appear.
- Every item reuses p2-02's `Sourced | NoSource` for each number: price band, store and supplier counts, velocity, and best margin. Best margin is always `NoSource` ("The landed-cost engine arrives with UX-8.").
- Each item also carries `first_seen_at`, the oldest family's `as_of` and `stale`.
- The page carries `next_cursor`, `as_of` and `degraded`: the blocked, disabled or stale sources that feed it.

**Momentum** reads G7 from `product_daily_features.growth_7d` and never recomputes it. It takes the latest day and the best-graded family: `shp_inv` (A) > `shp_bump` (B) > `cj_stock`. With no qualifying row it's `NoSource`, shown as "Insufficient signal" and sorted last.

**Web.**
- Two views: a grid (default, 2 to 6 columns) and a dense table. The table has a sticky header, a pinned name column, tabular right-aligned numbers, and renders only the rows in view.
- `V` or the segmented control switches views. The last view is kept per viewer, and view, sort and filters live in the URL.
- Every number is a `<Metric>` with its provenance card.
- A card or row opens `/products/[id]` by click or Enter.
- Both views load more by cursor as you scroll, and loaded cards and rows never animate.
- Below 768 px the filters open in a bottom sheet (`Filters (n)`).
- No Phase 4 score values.

**States:**
- loading skeletons (cards or rows) and loading more;
- empty with no data (links Data Health);
- empty under filters (one Remove button per active filter, plus Clear filters);
- degraded banner, gap chip, and stale and paused cues on each card;
- a stale line when a refresh fails;
- a first-load error with Retry;
- a next-page error that keeps every loaded card.
