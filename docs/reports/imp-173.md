# imp-173 + imp-155 + imp-175 — TapTooltip: a press keeps the reason, one reason at a time, Retry's reason below

**Agent:** Claude (Orca worker, `task_a5211d1decc1`, dispatch `ctx_a27f3773db84`) · **Branch:** `CodySpooner/imp-173-taptooltip-batch` · **Decision:** DEC-232 (proposed (imp-173)) · **Vault:** Agents/Improvement Backlog rows imp-173, imp-155, imp-175; Features/Data Health · **Sources:** `docs/reports/imp-153.md` leftovers 1 and 3, `docs/reports/imp-134.md` leftover 3

## Summary

All three fixes are in `TapTooltip` (`apps/web/src/components/data-health/atoms.tsx`). Every caller routes through it, so no call site needed a guard of its own.

1. **imp-173: a press opens or keeps the reason and never closes it.** This covers Enter, Space, a click and a tap. Escape, Tab away, a press anywhere else, the pointer leaving and another reason opening still close it. Radix closes a tooltip on a press of its own trigger in two places, and both are now stopped:
   - The trigger's `pointerdown` handler. `onOpenChange` ignores a close requested during a `pointerdown` on the trigger. It reads `window.event`, as DEC-213 already does for scroll.
   - The content's dismissable layer, which treats the trigger as outside the hint. It acts on `pointerdown` for a mouse and on the `click` for touch. The content's `onPointerDownOutside` cancels that dismissal when the press is on the trigger.

   The `pressedOpen` ref and the toggle are gone. Status hints get the same rule: a tapped hint now closes when you tap somewhere else, not when you tap it again.
2. **imp-155: one reason at a time.** When a press opens a closed reason, it first sends `tooltip.open` on `document`. Radix sends that event on every hover or focus open, and every open tooltip closes when it hears it. That's one line, and it also closes plain Radix `Tooltip`s. A module store or context would have been more code and would only cover `TapTooltip` (DEC-232 b). The event name is internal to Radix, so the new spec counts open reasons and fails if Radix renames it.
3. **imp-175: Retry's reason sits below Retry.** `TapTooltip` takes an optional `side`, passed through to `TooltipContent`. The `PanelError` and `StaleLine` Retry pass `bottom`, at every width. Radix still flips the reason above when there's no room below. Every other call site keeps Radix's default, top.

Nothing changed about what is locked, why, or the copy.

Each guard in (1) has its own failing check. I removed each one in turn and ran the spec. Without the `pointerdown` guard, the click-after-hover states read `closed, instant-open, closed, instant-open`. Without `onPointerDownOutside`, the same happens, and the second tap on a hint closes and reopens it.

For imp-155, the only path I could reproduce was a keyboard press while another reason was open on hover. A pointer press already closed the other reason in Chromium, through its dismissable layer and the blur. The fix covers all four ways a reason opens (tap, click, hover, keyboard), and the spec asserts 1 open reason after each.
