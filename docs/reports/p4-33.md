## Behaviour

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **`GET /v1/boards/{id}/items?format=csv`** | Every card in board order (`position`, then `product_id`), ignoring `cursor` and `limit`, as `pulso_board_<slug>_<date>.csv` ("Q4 gifts: <$40!" gives `q4-gifts-40`). Another workspace's board is a 404 (`owned()` runs first). It streams through `csv_response`: count first, the 50k cap, 500 cards a fetch, the BOM, `Z` times, dollars, the formula guard. |
| **Columns** | `position, product_id, product, price_min, price_max, currency, best_margin, best_margin_pct, trend, lifecycle, saturation, note, merged_into_id, added_at, price_as_of, best_margin_as_of, trend_as_of, lifecycle_as_of, saturation_as_of, stale`. Cells come from `with_stats()`, so they equal the JSON card's. A missing stat or as-of is an empty cell, never 0. `position` is the 1-based place the table's `#` shows. |
| **Board page** | Export CSV ends the Grid/Table row. `E` exports while focus is on the cards; never from a note, a card's `…` menu or with modifiers. Preparing, over-cap and failure with Retry are `ExportCsv`'s states (DEC-316, DEC-345). |
| **Add to board** | Icon on each Discover card (over the image, beside ★) and table row; labelled button after Watch on the Product Page. The popover lists active boards in the API's order (newest activity first, so the last-used board is on top and highlighted) with card counts, then New board. Typing filters by name. A free name offers New board "name", one Enter to create and add. A taken name (409; archived boards count) keeps the field and says why. |
| **After a pick** | The popover closes, focus returns to its button, and the toast says "Added to <board>" or "Already on <board>" with Open board. A failure says why, with Retry. Board lists refetch at once, so the next open shows the new order. |
| **`B`** | On Discover, opens the picker of the card or row holding focus (`[data-product]`). On the Product Page, opens the header's picker from anywhere. Never with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt, never while typing. |

**Keys and clicks for the add flow:**
- **Discover, product focused:** **B, Enter** (2 keys) adds to the most recent board; **B, ↓, Enter** (3) to the second.
- **Mouse:** **2 clicks**, the button then the board.
- **A new board:** B, type the name, Enter. One Enter creates the board and adds the product.
- **Product Page:** B from anywhere, then Enter.
- **Before:** there was no way to add a product to a board outside the board page.

**Board CSV:** **1 click** (Export CSV), or **E** while a card has focus. Before, there was no export.

**Tables with Export CSV in the UI:** Discover, Testing Board, Screener and now Boards (3 → **4**).
