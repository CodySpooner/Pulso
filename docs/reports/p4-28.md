## UI bar

- **styleseed-design-review:** **87 → 96 / 100 (A)** in two rounds.
  - **Round 1 findings, all fixed:**
    1. The caption "Spend vs stop-loss $48.66" read as if $48.66 were the spend. It's now "Stop-loss $48.66".
    2. The can't-run box had no next step. It now says "Can't compare with today's numbers: …" and links the Profit tab.
    3. Rows without a delta had an empty right slot and a bare "—". They now say "Re-quoted", "No source yet" or "New since start".
  - **Left over (backlog rows below):** the budget sentence in the drawer is dense; a card with orders whose spend is past the line shows a full neutral bar, which reads heavy in light theme.
- **States:** the card has no-break-even and no-signal states. The comparison has loading, error, stale, empty ("No changes since start."), can't-run and no-source-yet states, plus "as of".
- **Keyboard and motion:** cards and drawer are keyboard-only with a visible ring, and focus returns to the card on close (new). The chip fades with `duration.base` / `ease.out` through `motion/react-m` under the app's `LazyMotion` and `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`. The bar is static: no fake liveness.
- **Contrast:** AA was measured by `contrastMisses` (dark and light) on the Testing column and the comparison. The chips are 4.85:1 to 8.93:1.
- **Tokens only**, and no horizontal scroll at 375 px (asserted on every screenshot).
