## Leftovers (candidate backlog rows)

1. **Trends pane toggles and Sellers filter chips** are toggle chips, not segmented controls, so `segment-thumb` doesn't apply. The pane toggle's pressed state reads from its filled swatch, which now clears 3:1 on `secondary` (above). But its pressed background (`secondary` on `card`, 1.07) and border (`border-strong`) aren't 3:1 on their own. The Sellers chip's pressed border (`brand/50`) isn't in `uiPairs`. Candidate: P4, measure both chips against WCAG 1.4.11 and add their pairs to `uiPairs`.
2. **Profit at 375 px:** with no dim, the only visual recompute cue is the hairline at the top of the tab, which can be scrolled out of view while the slider is in use. Answers arrive within about 120 ms plus a round trip, so this was left. Candidate: P4, a cue next to the breakdown (for example a hairline on the "Per order" card) if slow answers are seen.
3. **"View as table" for Trends charts** (the optional half of B4, vault Accessibility): skipped. The lines now clear 3:1. Candidate: P4, a disclosure that renders the chart's day data as a table.
4. `--segment-mark` resolves to `--brand`. If the CVD-safe palette (Color & Tokens, pending) ever overrides `--brand`, the mark follows it.
