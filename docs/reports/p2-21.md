## Chosen rules (DEC-257)

- **Service:** each call takes an actor (`owner` or a user id) and a reason, takes the matching lock, flushes, and leaves the commit to the caller (UX-1's endpoints). Every changed `listings.product_id` gets one `match_decisions` row in the same transaction, and a moved listing's `match_confidence` becomes NULL.
  - **merge(A, into=B):** both ids follow `merged_into_id`, and the same product raises. A gets `merged_into_id` = B and `status='merged'`. Every listing on A, or on a product merged into A earlier, moves with action `merge`, one row each. Pending pairs the move puts on one product become `merged`.
  - **split(listings, into=None):** listings of one product move onto `into` or onto a new product named after the older listing, with action `split`. At least one listing stays, because moving a whole product is a merge. Existing candidates between moved and staying listings become `split`.
  - **accept(candidate):** two products merge, the younger (`first_seen_at`, then id) into the older, and the candidate becomes `merged`. Otherwise product-less listings link (action `accept`) and the candidate becomes `accepted`. Pending candidates only.
  - **reject(candidate):** one `reject` row per listing with the pointer unchanged, and the candidate becomes `rejected`. Pending candidates only.
- **The matcher holds splits:** both passes skip a pair when one listing has a `split` decision from the other listing's canonical product (or a product merged into it). A split is therefore a cannot-link against the old product, read from the append-only log. No placeholder candidate rows are needed, and a listing that joins the old product later isn't paired with the split one either.
- **A reject holds its pair only:** `manual()` skips `reject` rows. Otherwise one rejected pair would freeze, say, a CJ listing against every store and send all its matches to the queue. The `rejected` candidate and DEC-249 h's third-listing veto still keep the pair apart.
- **Two-product pairs** stay pending for the queue; a human accept merges them.
- **One lock:** `pg_advisory_xact_lock(0x50554C534D415443)` is taken by each matcher transaction (per source listing, and per GTIN pair) and by each service call. Without it, a merge landing between the matcher reading a pointer and linking a listing could leave that listing on a merged product. The bigint key can't collide with the workers' two-int Beat and source locks.
- **membership(t):** a `DISTINCT ON (listing_id)` replay of `match_decisions` with `decided_at < t`, latest first (ties by id). It never reads `listings.product_id` or `merged_into_id`. A listing without a decision before t is absent.
- **DEC-042 superseded (DEC-257 g):** from Phase 2, features that group listings by product read membership(t) at their cutoff, and the leakage test treats a current-mapping read as a failure.

## Acceptance

- **After a manual split, a matcher run (score + GTIN) above the auto threshold leaves the pair split with no new algo decision:** `test_split_stays_split_through_score_and_gtin_reruns`. Three listings (a CJ cost and two stores) score 1.0 on image, title and pHash, and share GTIN `4006381333931`; the first run links all three. The owner splits one off: one `owner`/`split` row from the old product to a new one named after the listing, `match_confidence` NULL, and the linking candidate becomes `split`. The re-run, with both passes at 1.0, leaves every `match_candidates`, `match_decisions` and `products` row unchanged. A fourth listing then joins the old product by GTIN and image, and no candidate pairs it with the split listing.
- **Merge moves every listing and sets `merged_into_id`, one decision row per moved listing:** `test_merge_moves_every_listing_with_one_decision_each`. Three listings move, one of them from a product merged into the source earlier, each with an `owner`/`merge` row from its own product to the survivor. The staying listing gets no row. The source reads `merged`, the pending pair is settled as `merged` with `decided_at`, and merging a product that already resolves to the survivor raises.
- **membership(t) before a later merge returns the earlier grouping:** `test_membership_replays_decisions_before_t`. An auto-link, then a split, then a merge. Membership before the link is empty. After the link it is the auto-linked product, recomputed after the later split and merge and unchanged. After the split it is the split grouping. Now it is the merge survivor, and it equals each replayed listing's current pointer (R-15). A listing placed on a product without a decision never appears.
- **A re-run writes no rows:** the split, merge and accept tests compare every `match_candidates`, `match_decisions` and `products` row before and after a run.
- **Accept and reject:** `test_accept_merges_two_products_or_links_product_less_listings` (the older product survives; two product-less listings at a 10× markup form a new product with one `accept` row each; accepting twice raises) and `test_reject_holds_its_pair_but_frees_each_side` (one `reject` row per listing with NULL to NULL; a third listing links with one side and the other side stays out; rejecting twice raises).
- **Guards:** `test_split_refuses_mixed_or_emptying_sets` (two products, a whole product, a product-less listing, an empty list). `test_decisions_and_the_matcher_share_one_transaction_lock`: another connection can't take the lock until the service's transaction commits.

## Observations (not changed)

- **Nothing computes product features yet.** `product_daily_features` is written only by API tests, so there was no current-mapping fallback to remove. The first feature job should take membership(t); the leakage fixture can assert it.
- **Live views read the current pointer:** the product header, Sellers and Profit routers group by `listings.product_id`. That's right for "now" views, and membership(t) is for point-in-time features.
- **Workspace rows on merge:** `product_notes` and `product_tags` stay on the merged product's id. Product Matching says they resolve through `merged_into_id`, while the Matching Queue merge dialog moves them (deduplicated). UX-1 should settle this with its merge preview; until then, notes on a merged product only show if a read follows the redirect.
- **The redirects map is loaded once per run.** Under the lock that's safe, because a merge moves every listing of the merged product, so no pointer read later names a merged product. A product merged outside the service (by hand) would still leave its listings, which `merge()` moves on the next merge of that group.
- **Global lock:** an owner decision waits for at most one matcher transaction (one source listing or one GTIN), and the reverse. Per-product locks are the upgrade if decisions ever queue behind a run (`ponytail:` note in `lock()`).
- **`apart()` runs one indexed query per pair** where either listing sits on a product, next to the existing candidate lookup.
- **Membership is a full pass** over `match_decisions` per call (`ponytail:` note). Materialise it only if replay measures slow (R-15).
- **Workspace scope:** `listings`, `products`, `match_candidates` and `match_decisions` are global market data (no `workspace_id`), so there is nothing to scope.
