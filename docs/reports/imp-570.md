## Cause

imp-460 (DEC-351) put a Trend Score link in every Discover grid card (`Trend` in `apps/web/src/app/(app)/discover/feed-views.tsx`, `href /products/<id>#score-trend`). The boards specs focused the card with `cards(page).first().getByRole("link")`, which now resolves to two links: the title link (`Sunset projection lamp`, stretched over the card) and the Trend link. That is a Playwright strict-mode violation, so 4 runs failed in CI run 40261197325: `add-to-board.spec.ts` lines 22 and 83, and line 107 for both the dark and the light theme.

The card isn't broken. B finds the product through `closest("[data-product]")` (`AddToBoardHost` in `boards/add-to-board.tsx`), so it opens the card's picker from either link. Enter on the Trend link opens `#score-trend`, as imp-460 intended. Either link gets the card's `has-[a:focus-visible]` outline. No component change.
