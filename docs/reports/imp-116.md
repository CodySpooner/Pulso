### Why not just let the Column cell wrap (imp-117)

I measured it at 375px with the CJ fixture. The table region is 307px wide; Row takes 48px. Without wrapping, Column took 178px and Problem **80px**. With wrapping, Column's narrowest is its longest word, "cj_product_id", plus padding: 125px. That leaves Problem 134px (8.4rem), short of the 12rem target. Breaking names at underscores would reach about 189px and split identifiers. So below `sm` the name moves into the Problem cell instead: Problem now gets **259px (16.2rem)**. From `sm` up, wrapping isn't needed (Problem is ≥ 12rem at 640 and 768 with the name on one line), and letting it wrap squeezed the name onto 2 lines at 1440 (styleseed round 0, fixed).
