## Result

I found **7 findings: 2 at P2 and 5 at P3.** None is already queued. Another 14 observations are already owned elsewhere (see Duplicates), and 7 more were measured but not raised (see the end of that section).

The screens are already coherent:

- every text size is on the type scale;
- one radius applies per element role;
- one accent and one icon family are used;
- a normal state is shown neutral, and colour appears only on the 2 of 4 sources that need attention;
- axe reports **0** `color-contrast` and **0** `target-size` violations in 28 scans (2 widths × 2 themes, on every screen except loading);
- nothing animates under reduced motion.

The styleseed scores run from **91 to 99, so every screen gets an A**. What is left is concentrated in three places:

- **what moves while data loads:** the first load shifts the layout by 0.174 CLS at 1440 px, where the budget is under 0.1;
- **the sidebar's scroll edges:** a focus ring is clipped at the top edge, and a focused item fades at the bottom edge;
- **the run drawer at phone width:** facts are cut off with no way to read the rest, a table column is clipped, and counts are not grouped.

## Method

- **Fixtures only.** `mockAdminApi` (`apps/web/e2e/data-health/fixtures.ts`) answers every `/api/v1/admin/*` call, and the page clock is pinned to 2026-10-22 14:00 UTC.
  - Shopify is Blocked, CJ is Healthy, Google Trends is Overdue and Frankfurter FX is Disabled.
  - The import preview has 3 valid rows and 1 invalid row.
  - The `down`, `delayMs` and `sources: []` / `coverage: []` options produce the API-down, loading and empty states.
- **Environment.** `next dev` with the E2E auth bypass. Chromium at 1440×900 and 375×812, `reducedMotion: "reduce"`, dark and light themes.
  - Nothing else was involved: no live source, Clerk session, secret or env file, and no collector stack.
  - The repo config's port 3100 was held by another process (PID 55220), so the scratch configs used ports 3917 and 3918.
- **Harness.** A scratch Playwright spec and config, not committed and deleted from the worktree afterwards (as in aud-010 and imp-074). It appends computed-style measurements to JSONL:
  - **Type inventory:** every visible element with its own text gives its size, line height, weight and family. Each size is checked against the [[Typography]] scale (including the phone `body`, `title-3` and `display` sizes), and everything under 12 px is listed.
  - **Spacing:** every padding and gap value not on the [[Layout & Spacing]] steps.
  - **Control heights and radii.**
  - **Heading sizes.**
  - **Truncated text:** ellipsis with `scrollWidth > clientWidth`, and whether a `title` exposes the rest.
  - **Contrast and targets:** axe-core 4.14.0 `color-contrast`, `target-size` and `heading-order`. The engine is the copy already installed through `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y`, injected at run time with nothing installed. Its contrast check uses the computed colours of the rendered page, the same pairs the token suite checks.
  - **Focus walk:** a keyboard walk of 40 stops at 1440 and 30 at 375. Each focused element must have a visible outline of at least 2 px that no overflow ancestor clips.
  - **Motion:** `document.getAnimations()` still running under reduced motion.
  - **Layout shift:** CLS from a `PerformanceObserver` (`layout-shift`) across the first load, with every response held 2.5 s.
- **Scoring.** `styleseed-design-review` (7 categories, 100 points; A is 90 or more), deducting only for evidence cited by file and line. Focus, contrast and reduced motion are not styleseed categories, so they appear as separate measured columns.
