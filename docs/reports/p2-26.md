## What was built

- **API** (`apps/api/src/pulso_api/routers/product_suppliers.py`, its own router): `GET /v1/products/{id}/suppliers?country=US&cursor=&limit=`.
  - **One row per live supplier listing:**
    - platform, supplier name, country and link;
    - unit cost in the listing's currency;
    - `cost`, the full `ProfitCalc` for that supplier: US freight and its days, duty, landed cost, margin, R-16 `complete` + `missing[]`, rates and named sources;
    - rating (hundredths of a star) with its review count;
    - orders with their semantics (`lifetime`, or a window of N days);
    - stock, years active, MOQ, bulk tiers and warehouse country;
    - `in_stock`, last seen, `stale` and `as_of`.
  - **Picks:** Best margin is `best()` over in-stock rows, so complete rows only. Fastest shipping is the lowest max days among in-stock rows, ties on landed cost. Both are computed over the whole set, not the page.
  - **Order:** best margin first, then by landed cost, then rows with no landed cost. Known out-of-stock rows come last, so the first row is the pick.
  - Keyset cursor (422 `invalid_cursor` otherwise), 404 for an unknown product, 308 for a merged one with the query string kept.
- **Suppliers tab** (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/products/[id]/suppliers/`):
  - **Pick cards:** Best margin (margin with sign and ▲/▼, $ per order before ads, landed cost, days) and Fastest shipping (days at twice the unit's size, carrier, landed cost, margin). Each has "Open in Profit". With no pick, the card says why in words, for example "Every supplier is out of stock." or "No supplier has complete costs. Needs a sell price and a freight quote."
  - **Dense table:** sticky header, sticky supplier column, tabular right-aligned numbers, 13 columns (supplier with pick and "Out of stock" badges, unit cost, ship to US, days, landed cost, margin before ads, rating, orders, years, MOQ with a bulk-tier popover, warehouse with a "US warehouse" mark, last seen, and actions).
    - Every number opens its source card by hover, focus or tap. A computed total shows its formula and inputs.
    - A number nothing backs reads "No source yet".
    - A withheld margin shows a short reason ("— Freight not quoted") and the full reasons in words on hover, focus or tap.
  - **Below 768 px:** one card per supplier, with landed cost and margin large and a full-width "Open in Profit".
  - **Notes on every row's gaps:** when every row lacks the market price (DEC-261) or the duty rate, one note says so with the fix ("Enter your price on Profit", "Set a US duty rate in Settings"). A stale-inputs note names each source and its last capture, with a link to Data Health.
  - **States:** loading skeleton (pick cards + 3 table rows), empty ("No supplier matched yet" with the Data Health CSV import), error with cause, error ID and Retry, a stale line on a failed refresh, and Load more.
- **Opening a supplier in Profit:** a row click, `Enter` on a focused row, or either "Open in Profit" button writes `?supplier=<listing id>` by `history.replaceState` (as the tab bar does) and switches to Profit.
  - The Profit tab reads `supplier=` and sends it as `supplier_listing_id`.
  - Its footnote reads "Costed from the … listing you opened from Suppliers", with "Let Pulso pick the supplier", which removes the parameter.
  - Back on Suppliers, that row has a static brand edge and an "In Profit" badge.
- **Keyboard:** `j`/`k` or arrows move between rows, `Enter` opens the row in Profit, `o` opens the listing in a new tab. Every control has a visible focus ring. The hint line shows the keys from 768 px up.
- **Motion:** the two pick cards fade in once (opacity, 200 ms, 40 ms stagger), and nothing in the table animates. Reduced motion is honoured through the global `MotionConfig` and CSS rule.

## Click count

- **Product page to a supplier's profit:** 2 clicks. (1) The Suppliers tab, then (2) that supplier's "Profit" button, its row, or a pick card's "Open in Profit". With the keyboard: focus a row, then `Enter`.
- **Back to Pulso's own pick:** 1 click ("Let Pulso pick the supplier").
- **A number's source:** 0 clicks (hover or focus), or 1 tap on touch.
