### Candidates (DEC-281 c)

**Inputs.** The product name plus every listing title. Titles that differ only in case or spacing count once.

**Phrases.** A candidate is a run of 1–4 words. Runs break at:
- stop words, including listing noise such as "new", "hot", "sale", "free shipping" and "pcs";
- punctuation, and a dash with spaces around it;
- single characters, pure numbers and quantities (`2pcs`, `50ft`, `5000mah`).

A hyphen inside a word stays (`anti-slip`). A trailing plural folds into the singular through `taxonomy.fold`, and each phrase shows the spelling most titles use.

**Relevance.** It is the average of two shares, times a length weight:
- the share of titles that hold the phrase;
- the mean share of titles that hold each of its words. Titles often reorder the same words, so this term matters.
- Length weights: 1 word 0.5, 2 words 0.85, 3–4 words 1.0. These are guesses.

**What is shown.** A phrase that only ever appears inside one longer phrase, with the same evidence, is left out. The best 12 that are not yet decided are shown.

### API behaviour (DEC-281 d)

**Reading (GET).**
- `relevance` comes from the current titles; it is null when no title holds the keyword. `as_of` is the newest listing `last_seen_at`.
- Order: relevance, typed-in keywords last, then A to Z.

**Writing (POST).**
- The server normalizes the keyword: casefolded, curly apostrophe made straight, spacing collapsed. It must be 1–100 characters with at least one letter or digit, and extra fields fail.
- `source` is derived on the server: `title` when a title holds the keyword.
- A plural or case variant of a decided keyword decides that keyword.
- Repeating the newest decision writes nothing, so `decided_at` keeps its time.

**Merges.**
- Writing to a merged id answers 409 `merged`.
- Reads follow `merged_into_id` at any depth, and the newest decision per keyword wins (DEC-263).

### UI and click count

- **Confirming a keyword: 1 click** on the Trends tab ("Confirm"). From the product page it is **2 clicks**: the Trends tab, then Confirm. Rejecting is also 1 click. Adding a keyword is typing it and pressing Enter.
- **Confirmed keywords** are chips. Only a typed-in one carries a mark (a pen icon, read aloud as "Added by you"). The × rejects, and the decision stays on record.
- **Suggested rows** show a relevance bar and a tabular number, with Confirm and Reject. The hover shows the relevance's source and as of. A **Rejected (n)** disclosure offers "Confirm instead".
- **No-source line:** a muted "Search interest per keyword · No source yet" line.
- **Saves:**
  - They are optimistic and built on the query cache, not the render, so a quick second click never undoes the first (imp-278).
  - Only the newest answer replaces the list.
  - A failure rolls back, says "Couldn't save the keyword. Your change was undone" with the error ID, and refetches.
- **Focus:** it moves to the row that takes the decided row's place, then the row before it, then the field.
- **States:** loading skeleton, error with Retry, stale line (a refresh failed while the list stays), nothing to suggest, failed save, and no source yet.
- **Motion:** no new motion. Press feedback is the existing transform-only button scale.
