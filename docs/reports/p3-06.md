### The weekly set (DEC-292 a–c)

**Order.**
1. The approved seeds, in seed order.
2. With the setting on, the confirmed keywords: most workspaces confirming, then the earliest confirmation (`decided_at` of the current confirmed decision), then A to Z.

**Rules.**
- **Duplicates:** keywords match on the casefolded, whitespace-collapsed form. The first spelling wins, so a seed keeps its spelling and its place.
- **Which decision counts:** a workspace's newest decision per keyword across a product and the products merged into it (DEC-263). A rejection on the survivor therefore beats an older confirmation on the merged product.
- **The cap:** `TRENDS_MONTHLY_SEARCH_LIMIT // 5`. A keyword past it, or a confirmed keyword with a comma, is left out of the plan. It gets no target row and no retry.
- **Seeds:** with the setting on, seeds count toward the cap too. A seed with a comma still raises, as before.
- **Setting off:** the plan is today's seed-only plan, uncapped.

**Projected SerpApi usage at the cap.**

| Monthly limit | Weekly cap | Four-Monday month | Five-Monday month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 250 (default) | 50 | 200 | **250 ≤ 250** |
| 49 | 9 | 36 | 45 |

- **Retries:** DEC-157 retries still reserve from the monthly budget. In a five-Monday month at a full set, a retry makes `reserve` stop the run partial before the 251st search, so the hard cap holds.
- **Hourly limit:** a full 50-keyword run fills the hourly limit of 50 exactly (DEC-154). Any retry inside that run hits the hourly limit, so the run stops partial and the last keyword waits for next week. This is existing behaviour, not new; see p3-06-b3.

**What isn't folded.** Plural folding (`lamps` is `lamp`) stays in the API. Two plural twins decided separately on two products before a merge count as two searches. The code says so in a `ponytail:` comment, and `pulso_sources` can't import the API's tokenizer.

### API (DEC-292 d)

- **Tracked:** each confirmed keyword gets `tracked` = its normal form is in `weekly_set(...).keywords`. Candidates and rejected keywords get `null`.
- **Reason:** `untracked_reason` is `disabled` when the setting is off, `comma` when the keyword has one, and otherwise `budget`.
- **Budget block:** `trends` gives `weekly_used`, the number of keywords in the set (seeds plus every workspace's), with `weekly_cap`, `monthly_limit`, `enabled`, and `as_of` (the time of the count, required by the provenance contract).
- **Same function:** the page and the job both call `weekly_set`. `test_tracked_is_exactly_the_weekly_plan` checks every confirmed keyword's `tracked` against `plan()` membership on the same rows, with the setting on and off.

### UI and click count (DEC-292 e)

- **Cost line**, under the add field. It is the accessible description of the field and of every Confirm and "Confirm instead", so a screen reader hears it before the click.
  - **Room:** "Each tracked keyword is 1 SerpApi search a week, about 4–5 of the 250 a month. **12 of 50 weekly slots used.**" A thin neutral meter sits at its left.
  - **Full:** "**50 of 50 weekly slots used.** A keyword you confirm now is saved but not tracked until a slot frees." The meter turns amber; the text says it too.
  - **Off:** "**Trends tracking is off.** Confirmed keywords are saved, but nothing is fetched for them." No slot numbers show.
  - **Hover:** what was counted and when, plus the Monday 05:00 UTC slot and the monthly limit. When off, it names the setting.
- **Marker**, on each confirmed chip.
  - A filled neutral dot means tracked; a hollow ring means not tracked. Shape carries the difference, never colour alone.
  - The marker's accessible name is "Tracked" or "Not tracked: weekly set full / tracking is off / has a comma". The chip's hover gives the full reason above the relevance provenance.
  - The Confirmed heading explains the dot once: "Confirmed · 2 · ● 2 tracked".
- **In flight:** a keyword shows no marker until the API answers. The optimistic update keeps the budget block.
- **Clicks.** Confirming a suggestion is **1 click** once the Trends tab is open (2 from the product page), as in KW-1. Adding is type plus Enter (or Add): **1 action**. Nothing extra stands between the owner and confirming; the cost is visible beforehand without a dialog.
- **States:**
  - loading, error with Retry, stale, empty and failed save are unchanged from KW-1;
  - "no source yet" is the off line plus the existing "Search interest per keyword · No source yet";
  - nothing animates (no motion added), and keyboard focus is unchanged: the focus shot shows the ring on Confirm.
