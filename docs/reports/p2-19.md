## Chosen rules (DEC-255)

- **Validation:** strip whitespace and dashes. Accept 8, 12, 13 or 14 ASCII digits, left-pad to 14, and check the GS1 mod-10 digit. Rejected even with a valid check digit:
  - placeholders: all zeros, one repeated digit, or an ascending or descending run once leading zeros are dropped (`123456789012`, `1234567890128`, `012345678905` and `987654321098` all carry valid check digits);
  - GS1 Restricted Circulation Numbers: GS1 prefixes 020–029, 040–049 and 200–299, and GTIN-8 prefixes 0 and 2. GS1 lets companies and regions issue these internally, so two Shopify stores with sequential "in-store" barcodes would otherwise collide.
- **Groups:** every barcode is normalised and grouped by GTIN-14. A vendor (a store or a supplier) that puts one GTIN on two or more of its listings drops out of that GTIN. That rules out same-store pairs and stops one store's reused code from pulling its catalogue onto one product.
- **Precedence:** the pass runs before candidate generation. Each cross-vendor pair gets a `match_candidates` row at score 1.0 with `scorer_version` `gtin-1`, or an existing `pending` row is raised to it, keeping its image components as evidence. M-3 never re-scores a `gtin-1` row, so a GTIN pair is never lowered to its image score and re-runs don't flip it back and forth. There is no price gate.
- **Link and manual rules:** each pair goes through M-3's `link()`, so DEC-249 g and h apply unchanged:
  - only product-less listings move;
  - two listings on different products stay `pending` for M-5;
  - a manually decided listing never moves;
  - a rejected or split pair is never touched, and is never linked through a third listing.
  - Decisions carry actor `algo`, confidence 1.0000 and reason `gtin-1: GTIN <14 digits>`.

## Acceptance

- **Two fixture listings sharing a barcode in different formats link at 1.0:** `test_shared_gtin_links_across_formats_at_confidence_one_and_reruns_add_nothing`. UPC-A `036000291452` on a store listing and EAN-13 `0036000291452` on a supplier listing. Both have no embeddings, and the prices are ones M-3's gate calls insane. Both end on one new product named after the older listing. Each listing has one decision: `algo`, `link`, from NULL, confidence 1.0000, reason `gtin-1: GTIN 00036000291452`, the `candidate_id` of an `accepted` row at 1.0000 / `gtin-1`. `match_confidence` is 1.0000.
- **Invalid check digits and placeholders don't link:** `test_invalid_placeholder_and_reused_gtins_never_link` covers a wrong check digit, `123456789012`, an RCN (`212345678909`) and a store reusing one GTIN on two listings. It ends with no candidate, no decision and no product.
- **A re-run writes no rows:** the first and third tests compare every `match_candidates`, `match_decisions` and `products` row before and after a second run.
- **A manual reject or split is honoured:** `test_gtin_pairs_honour_manual_decisions_and_outrank_image_scores[rejected|split]`.
  - The decided row is byte-identical after the run.
  - A third listing sharing the pair's GTIN joins one side, and the other side's pair with it stays `pending` at 1.0.
  - An owner-unlinked listing never moves.
  - A two-product pair stays `pending` at 1.0 / `gtin-1` even though M-3 sees it in its queue band.
  - A pair M-3 had queued at 0.83 is raised to 1.0 and linked.
- **`MATCHER_ENABLED` off writes nothing:** the pass is inside `match()`, which the job calls only when the flag is on. `test_flag_off_logs_and_never_opens_the_database` (unset and `off`) shows the session is never opened.

## Observations (not changed)

- **Only Shopify stores barcodes today.** `shopify_stores/variants.py` (product `.js`) writes `listing_variants.barcode`. Neither CJ writer (`cj/tracked.py`, `cj/csv.py`) does, so GTIN links are store-to-store until a supplier adapter keeps one. The Shopify `.js` fixture's barcode `0012345678905` is the textbook example UPC, and the placeholder rule rejects it.
- **Full read per run:** the pass reads every non-null barcode into memory (a `ponytail:` note in `gtins()`). Grouping a normalised column in SQL is the upgrade once `listing_variants` grows.
- **UPC-E:** an 8-digit UPC-E reads as a GTIN-8, and its leading 0 makes it an RCN-8, so it never links (a missed link, never a false one). Expanding UPC-E to UPC-A is a possible recall gain if real data shows stores entering UPC-E.
- **Workspace scope:** `listings`, `listing_variants`, `match_candidates`, `match_decisions` and `products` are global market data (no `workspace_id`), so there is nothing to scope.
