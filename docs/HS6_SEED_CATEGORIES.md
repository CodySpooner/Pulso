# Seed CJ categories → HS-6 candidates

**Purpose:** imp-005 / audit D-01 maps all 10 `cj_categories` names in [seed_v1.yaml](../infra/seeds/seed_v1.yaml) to candidate commodity groups for the proposed Census International Trade adapter (D-02). **This mapping feeds no score or feature until a DEC adopts a trade source.** It does not change Pulso's product taxonomy (DEC-043), seeds, source flags or runtime behavior.

**Sources and date checked:** 2026-10-22, public reference documents only. Descriptions below come from the Census [2026 Schedule B chapters](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2026/index.html), whose index specifies use after July 1, 2026. Its [Introduction, p. i](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/schedules/b/2026/introduction.pdf#page=1) confirms that chapters 1–97 share their first six digits and descriptions with HTS. Only those international six-digit **subheadings** are used here; two digits identify the chapter and four the heading. Export-specific ten-digit suffixes are not import codes. The [Census trade datasets guide, p. 16](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/reference/guides/Guide_to_International_Trade_Datasets.pdf#page=16) documents HS6 aggregation; no data API was called.

**Interpretation:** these are analyst-inferred, non-exhaustive product-family candidates, guided by the seed keywords, not an official CJ crosswalk or SKU/customs classifications. Official description excerpts retain parent scope where the subheading says “Other.” Category assignment and examples are our interpretation. Confidence measures category fit: **high** would mean a narrow category with little ambiguity; **medium** means identifiable core families with construction-dependent gaps; **low** means a broad or mixed category with substantial omissions or unrelated trade in the same codes. No whole category earns high confidence without product specifications.

**Review/use boundary:** 10/10 categories have a candidate list checked against the cited references; independent Claude cross-review remains pending. Do not treat category rows as disjoint or sum them as a market total: `850760` overlaps Gadgets/Phones and `901910` overlaps Beauty/Fitness. Before D-02 uses a list, review actual product materials/functions and exclusions, select its scope, and check code validity/concordances for each historical year. Broad imports are macro context, not measured dropshipping sales; the 2026 list alone does not establish historical coverage.
